## 1. Create your account

Go to [app.softbooq.com](https://app.softbooq.com) and sign up. During onboarding you will be asked to:

- Enter your company name and industry
- Invite your first team members
- Select the modules you want to activate

## 2. Configure your workspace

Once inside the app, head to **Settings** to complete your workspace setup:

- **Company profile** — logo, address, currency, timezone
- **Fiscal year** — set your accounting period start date
- **Email** — connect your email provider for sending invoices and quotes
- **Users & Roles** — invite team members and assign permissions

## 3. Activate your first module

Start with the module most relevant to your immediate workflow:

| Goal | Start here |
|------|------------|
| Send invoices and track payments | [Finance](/erp/finance) |
| Manage customers and deals | [CRM](/erp/crm) |
| Track employees and payroll | [HR](/erp/hr) |
| Manage stock and warehouses | [Inventory](/erp/inventory) |
| Run projects and track time | [Projects](/erp/projects) |

## 4. Explore how modules connect

Visit the **System Map** inside the app (sidebar, bottom) to see a visual diagram of how every module connects and how data flows across your ERP.
