<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Go to [accounts.softbooq.com/signup](https://accounts.softbooq.com/signup). Sign-up has three steps:

    - **Organization**: your company name, a **workspace handle**, your country, and the **currency of your books**
    - **Account**: your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters)
    - **Plan**: Softbooq Go, Plus or Pro

    Your handle becomes the address of your public site, `<your-handle>.mysoftbooq.com`, and you can change it later. A name with ä, ö, ü or ß suggests a handle with ae, oe, ue or ss. Your country sets your tax and invoice defaults.

    <Warning>
      The currency of your books cannot be changed later. Your accounts, reports and invoices are kept in it.
    </Warning>

    Every workspace starts with a 30-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial, and you can switch plans as often as you like. See [Subscription & Plans](/billing/subscription).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up with Jacinta, or with the forms">
    When you start setting up, Softbooq asks how you would like to do it:

    - **Set up with AI**: tell Jacinta about your business in your own words. Your workspace fills in as you talk, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. Setup with Jacinta is free and ends when setup is finished.
    - **Set up myself with the forms**: about ten minutes.

    Either way you see every answer before it is saved. The setup covers, step by step: **Your books**, **Invoicing**, **Branding**, **Team**, **Sample data**, **Customers and products**, **Bank**, **Opening balances**, **Invoice identity**, **Access**, and **Jacinta**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Try things in Practice first (optional)">
    Your **Practice workspace** is a copy of your workspace with example customers, products and invoices in your currency. Try anything there: nothing is real and nothing is sent. Emails it would have sent wait in **Would have been sent**.

    Find it any time in the workspace switcher. It starts again fresh every week, and your real records stay separate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send email from your own domain (optional)">
    Softbooq sends your invoices, reminders and notifications from its own mail service from day one, within a monthly allowance of free emails. To send from your own domain with no limit, open **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service** and choose **Your own domain**, **Brevo** or **Custom SMTP (advanced)**, then use **Test Connection**. See [Sending email](/integrations/email).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **User Management** and invite your first team members.

    Roles you can give:

    | Role | What they can do |
    |------|----------------|
    | **Admin** | Everything, including billing, users and security |
    | **Manager** | Runs the business modules, including Finance |
    | **Employee** | Day-to-day work in the modules their role allows; no Finance |
    | **Viewer** | Looks without changing anything; no Finance |
    | **Sales Rep** | CRM and Sales: customers, quotes and orders. Cannot raise an invoice or take payment |
    | **Cashier** | The register only. Does not use a paid seat |
    | **Auditor** | Read-only access to the books, for an outside accountant. Does not use a paid seat; at most two |
    | **AI User** | Softbooq AI only. Does not use a paid seat |

    What each role may do can be adjusted in **User Management → Roles & Permissions**. Customers are not users: they sign in to their [customer account](/portals/customer-account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start with the module you need most">
    Which modules you have depends on your plan (see [Subscription & Plans](/billing/subscription)). Pick the one that solves your most pressing problem first.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Send invoices and get paid" icon="file-invoice" href="/erp/finance">
        Finance: invoices, payments, expenses and your books.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Sell at the counter and online" icon="cash-register" href="/erp/sales">
        Sales & POS: orders, the register and your online store.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Keep track of stock" icon="boxes-stacked" href="/erp/inventory">
        Products & Services: products, stock and transfers.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Buy from suppliers" icon="cart-shopping" href="/erp/procurement">
        Procurement: suppliers and purchase orders.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="See how everything connects">
    Open **System Map** at the bottom of the sidebar for a live diagram of the modules and how records flow between them.
  </Step>

</Steps>
