Softbooq is a modern business platform built for growing companies. It brings together ERP, cloud storage, and AI tools into a single unified workspace — so your team spends less time switching between tools and more time doing the work that matters.

## How the docs are organised

**ERP** covers all 17 modules — Finance, HR, CRM, Sales, Procurement, Inventory, and more. Each module page explains what it owns, what tasks it handles, and how it connects to the rest of the system.

**Files** covers the standalone cloud storage app at files.softbooq.com — connecting SharePoint, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, and local folders into one interface.

**Public & Portals** covers the four public-facing surfaces every Softbooq workspace gets: a Landing Page, an Online Storefront, a Client Portal, and a Careers Portal.

If you're new, start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) guide to get your workspace set up in under 15 minutes.
