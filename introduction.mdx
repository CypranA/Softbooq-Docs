Softbooq runs your whole business on one set of records: sales and the register, invoices and your books, stock, purchasing, people and payroll, projects and more. Jacinta, Softbooq AI's assistant, helps in every part of it. It works in English, Deutsch, Español and Français.

New here? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## The apps

One sign-in, at `accounts.softbooq.com`, opens all of them, and they all work on the same records.

## Your public site

Every workspace has a public site at `<your-handle>.mysoftbooq.com`: a landing page, an online shop, a careers page, a help centre, document verification, contract signing, and a customer account where your customers sign in with an emailed code. See [Landing page](/portals/landing-page).

## How these docs are organised

- **ERP**: one page per module: what it does, the common tasks, and how it connects to the others.
- **Public site**: your landing page, shop, careers page and customer account.
- **Integrations**: the tools Softbooq connects to, and where to set each one up.
- **Platform**: your account, the Admin Portal, security and Softbooq AI.
- **Billing**: plans and AI credits.
