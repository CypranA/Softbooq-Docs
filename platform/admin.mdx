Running a workspace happens in two places. The **Admin Portal**, at `accounts.softbooq.com/admin`, holds billing, seats and apps. **User Management**, **Audit Logs** and **Settings** in the ERP hold roles, security and everything else about how the workspace works.

## The Admin Portal

Only Admins can open it: from your account page, choose **Admin Portal**, or **Manage** next to a workspace. If you are Admin of several workspaces, pick one under **Workspace**.

| Section | What is there |
|---------|---------------|
| **Billing** | Your plan, its status, seats used and AI credits (**Top Up**). Change plan, monthly or yearly. **Manage Billing** shows your invoices and lets you change the payment method or cancel |
| **Seats** | Everyone in the workspace, with **Invite User**, **Add Seats**, **Change Role** and **Deactivate**. **Pending Invitations** can be copied, resent or revoked; an invitation link lasts 7 days |
| **Apps** | The Softbooq apps your workspace uses, such as Files (included free) and Softbooq AI and BI (paid for with credits) |
| **Settings** | **Billing email**, **VAT / Tax number**, **Country** and your address for invoices. Your company name and timezone are set in the ERP's Settings |
| **Integrations** | Your requests for integrations switched on by request, such as Xero |

## Users and roles in the ERP

**User Management** in the ERP has three tabs:

- **User List**: everyone in the workspace, their role and their active sessions
- **Roles & permissions**: what each role can do
- **Security policy**: for Admins, see below

The roles are Admin, Manager, Employee, Viewer, Cashier, Sales Rep, Auditor and AI User. Choosing Admin gives full access to billing and workspace settings.

Requests from people who asked to delete their account also arrive in User Management, for you to approve.

### View As

An Admin can choose **View As** on someone in the **User List** to see the ERP as that person sees it, for example to check what a new role can reach. A banner shows **Viewing as** and their name until you choose **Exit**. You stay signed in as yourself, and the start and end are written to the audit log.

## Security policy

In **User Management → Security policy**:

- **Two-factor authentication**: choose the **Roles that must use two-factor authentication** and a **Grace period (days)**. People in those roles are asked to set up two-factor authentication before they can carry on in the ERP.
- **Session Policy**: lock the screen on a computer after a period without activity. Phones are never locked for inactivity.
- **Workspace Access**: limit who can join to email addresses on your own domains, with exceptions you list. Set **Enforcement** to **Off**, **Warn** or **Enforced**.
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: enterprise single sign-on is available on request. Choose **Contact support**.

Saving asks you to confirm it is you.

## Audit Logs

**Audit Logs** records who did what in your workspace: records created, changed and deleted, sign-ins and sign-outs, settings and role changes, invitations, payment runs, actions Jacinta took for someone, and every **View As**. Entries cannot be edited or deleted, by anyone.

Filter by **Entity Type**, **Action**, **Performed By** and **Period**, and **Export** as CSV or JSON.

Audit Logs are part of Softbooq Pro. Admins, Managers and Auditors can see them.

## Closing the workspace

In the ERP, **Settings → Billing & Plans → Close this workspace**. Everyone loses access at once, you included, and the subscription is cancelled. Nothing is deleted straight away: the records are kept for 90 days, then the workspace and everything in it is permanently deleted, unless you reopen it first or a document in it is under legal hold. Its Admins get an email 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before the deletion date. Export any records you are required to keep before then.
