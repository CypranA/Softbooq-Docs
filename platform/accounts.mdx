Softbooq Accounts, at `accounts.softbooq.com`, is where you sign in and manage your own account. Sign in once there and you are signed in to every Softbooq app: the ERP, POS, Files, Softbooq AI and Softbooq BI.

## Signing in

There are no passwords. Sign in with:

- **Continue with Google**
- **Continue with Microsoft**
- your email address: choose **Use email instead** if needed, enter your address and **Continue**. We email you a 6-digit code to enter as the **Verification Code**, then **Verify**.

A short security check runs before the code is sent.

Someone invited to a workspace joins the same way.

## Two-factor authentication

Under **Security**, choose **Set Up Authenticator**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (or enter the key by hand), and enter the 6-digit code to **Verify & Enable**. From then on, Softbooq asks for a code from the app each time you sign in.

**Remove Authenticator** turns it off, and you sign in with your email code only. Your workspace can require two-factor authentication for some roles: see [Admin Portal and security](/platform/admin).

## Your account page

| Section | What is there |
|---------|---------------|
| **Home** | A checklist of what is left to set up, and your workspaces |
| **Profile** | Your photo, **Display Name**, **Phone Number**, **Timezone** and **Language** |
| **Security** | Two-factor authentication |
| **Your devices** | Everywhere you are signed in |
| **Workspaces** | The workspaces you belong to |
| **Privacy & Data** | Download your data, or delete your account |
| **Preferences** | **Appearance** (**Light**, **System** or **Dark**), your avatar colour, and how dates and times are shown |

## Your devices

**Your devices** lists every browser and device signed in to your account, with roughly where it is and when it was last active. Signing a device out ends it in every Softbooq app, and it loses access within the hour, even with a page open.

- **Sign out** next to a device signs out that device
- **Sign out other devices** keeps this one and ends the rest
- **Sign Out All Devices** ends every session, including this one

The email you get when you sign in has a **Sign out this device** link, which works once within 7 days.

Admins of a workspace you belong to can see the device and IP address of sessions used in their workspace, and can sign those sessions out.

## Workspaces

**Workspaces** lists every workspace you belong to, with its public site address. **Create workspace** starts a new one. Admins can **Manage** a workspace, which opens the Admin Portal. Anyone else can **Leave**.

## Your data and deleting your account

**Download Your Data** gives you a file with your profile, your workspace memberships, your employee records, your sessions and your recent activity.

**Delete Account** asks you to type your email to confirm.

- If you belong to workspaces, each one reviews the request itself, and your data is removed from a workspace once it approves. Records the law requires a business to keep, such as invoices, expenses and the audit log, are kept.
- If you belong to none, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

You can cancel the request until a workspace approves it.
