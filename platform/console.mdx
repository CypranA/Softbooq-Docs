Softbooq staff cannot see your business data. If you want support to look at something with you, a workspace Admin lets them in: they can read, never change anything, and only until the time you choose. You can end it at any time, and every visit is listed.

## Let support in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Legal & Compliance">
    Find the **Softbooq support access** card. Only workspace Admins can use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Let support read your data">
    In **What should support look at?**, say what the problem is, so support knows where to look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose For how long">
    **1 day**, **3 days** or **7 days**. Seven days is the longest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Let support read">
    The card shows **Support can read** and the date access ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## End it early

Choose **End access now**. Support can no longer read your data, and any support session that is open ends at once.

## See every visit

**History** lists each time support opened your workspace, so you can see exactly when someone looked.

## What support can and cannot do

- They can read your workspace's data, to understand the problem.
- They cannot change, add or delete anything.
- When the time you chose runs out, access ends by itself.
