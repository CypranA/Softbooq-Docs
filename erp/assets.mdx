The Assets module tracks your company's fixed assets — equipment, vehicles, furniture, and technology — through their full lifecycle from acquisition to disposal, with automatic depreciation posted to Finance.

## What Assets owns

- **Fixed assets** — the asset register with acquisition details and current book value
- **Depreciation** — scheduled and posted depreciation entries
- **Disposals** — records of sold or written-off assets

## Data flows out of Assets to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Depreciation entries |
| Maintenance | Asset maintenance logs |

## Data flows into Assets from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| IT & Helpdesk | IT asset tracking |

## Key features

### Asset register
Maintain a complete record of every fixed asset including purchase date, cost, location, assigned employee, and depreciation method.

### Depreciation
Choose straight-line or declining balance depreciation. The system calculates and posts depreciation entries to Finance automatically on schedule.

### Asset categories
Group assets by category (vehicles, equipment, IT hardware, etc.) for reporting and bulk depreciation configuration.

### Disposals
Record the sale or write-off of an asset. Automatically calculates gain or loss and posts the disposal entry to Finance.
