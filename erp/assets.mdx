Asset Management is your register of what the business owns, from laptops and vehicles to machines, buildings, software and intangible assets: who has it, where it is, what it is worth and how it is depreciated. It is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## How it is organised

Tabs: **Registry**, **Maintenance**, **Finance** and **Analytics**. At the top: **Register Asset**, **Scan Asset**, **Audit Mode**, **Print QR Labels** and the **Data** menu (export, backup, restore and **Bulk Import**).

---

## Common tasks

### Register an asset

Choose **Register Asset**. Pick a category (IT & Computing, Fleet & Vehicles, Furniture & Fittings, Machinery & Equipment, Facility & Building, Software & Licenses, Intangible Assets) and fill in its details, purchase, specifications and safety records. Name its custodian, the person who has it. Its location comes from **Settings → Business → Locations**.

For depreciation, choose the method (**Straight Line**, **Double Declining Balance** or **No Depreciation**), the useful life, the salvage value and the start date.

### Run depreciation

Depreciation is posted every month automatically. To post it now, open the **Finance** tab and choose **Run Depreciation**.

### Change an asset's value

From the asset's details you can record an impairment or a recovery (**Impair / recover (IAS 36)**), correct its cost, or reverse a capitalisation. Each posts to your books.

### Dispose of an asset

Choose **Dispose Asset**: scrapped, sold (with the sale proceeds), donated or other, with a reason. The disposal posts to your books. An asset that has been capitalised cannot be deleted; dispose of it instead. **Permanently Delete** is for records entered by mistake, and only Admins can use it.

### Check what you have

**Audit Mode** walks you through a stock-take, scanning QR labels with **Scan Asset**. **Print QR Labels** prints them.

### See what assets cost

**Analytics** shows the value of your assets over the next years and what they cost to own. The **Maintenance** tab creates work orders: see [Maintenance](/erp/maintenance).
