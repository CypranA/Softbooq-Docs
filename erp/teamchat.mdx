Team chat is where your team talks: one colleague to another, or a whole team in a channel. A question about an invoice is asked where the invoice is, and the invoice can sit in the conversation as a card. Team chat is part of every plan.

## How it is organised

Open **Team chat** in the sidebar, or the chat icon at the top of every page, which shows how many messages are waiting for you. On the left are **Your channels**, **Direct messages** and **Channels you can join**; the conversation you pick opens beside them.

Everyone whose role can see the team can use team chat; roles are set in **User management → Roles & permissions**. The Auditor and AI User roles do not have it: staff conversations are other people's personal data.

---

## Common tasks

### Message a colleague

Choose **New message**, then **Message a colleague**, and find them by name. Only the two of you can read a direct message. Admins cannot read it either.

### Start a channel

Choose **New message**, then **New channel**. Give it a name, for example "shop floor" or "month end", and, if you like, a line saying what it is for. Then choose who can read it:

- **Public channel**: everyone who uses team chat can find it, read it and join.
- **Private channel**: only the people in it can see it. Admins cannot read it either.

Anyone in a channel can add people under **Details and people**. The person who started it can remove someone, and so can an admin in a public channel. The person who started it, or an admin, can **Archive** the channel: nobody can write in it any more, and its messages stay readable to the people who could read them.

### Write, edit and remove

Press Enter to send, and Shift + Enter for a new line. You can edit your own messages; edited ones say so. Removing a message takes its words, files and cards away for everyone, and a line says a message was removed. An admin can remove any message; that removal is recorded in the activity log, without the words.

### Share a record as a card

Paste a link to an invoice, an order or a customer into a message, or choose **+** to put in a product, customer, invoice, quote or sale. It shows as a card. Each person opens it with their own access, so a card never shows more than its reader may see; someone without access sees only that it is not open to them.

### Send files

Attach up to 10 files to one message, each up to 25 MB. Only the people who can read the conversation can open them.

### Mention someone, and mute

Type @ and a name to mention a colleague. They hear about it with one alert per conversation, never one per message. In a direct message the other person gets that alert too. To stop the alerts and the unread count for a conversation, choose **Mute**.
