Books Health checks your books and tells you in plain words whether your figures can be trusted. Open it in **Finance → Dashboard → Books Health** (it is also under **Accounting**).

## What it tells you

<Frame caption="The answer at the top, then Fix in this order: each item says what is wrong and what to do, with Ask Jacinta beside it.">
  <img src="/images/erp/books-health.jpg" alt="Books Health showing 7 things to fix, the passed and to-review counts, and the first item with Ask Jacinta" />
</Frame>


At the top you see one of three answers:

- **Your books are in good shape**
- **Your books balance - N things to review**: nothing is wrong in the totals, but something deserves a look
- **N things to fix in your books**

Problems are listed under **Fix in this order**, and checks that passed under **Passed**. Anything it could not check is shown as **Not checked**, never counted as passed. Use **Check again** after a fix.

## What it checks

Among the checks:

- Your balance sheet adds up, your profit figure matches the balance sheet, and every account appears on a report
- Documents that never reached your accounts
- Supplier bills that are booked but still marked draft
- Employee expense claims booked as owed to a supplier
- Foreign-currency entries counted at the wrong exchange rate, or at a rate of exactly 1
- Paid shop orders that are not in your books, and shop orders with no cost of goods
- Documents whose VAT on your VAT return differs from your ledger
- Things in your books that have no account in your chart

## Repairs

Where Books Health can fix a problem, it offers the repair next to it, for one item or all of them (for example **Post it** or **Post all**). Before anything happens, a confirmation says exactly what the repair does: a missing journal entry, for instance, is written through the normal posting code with the document's own date and amounts, and if an entry already exists, nothing is posted.

To understand a problem first, choose **Ask Jacinta** next to it. See [Jacinta on problem screens](/erp/jacinta-help).
