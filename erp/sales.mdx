The Sales module handles the full order-to-cash cycle — from creating sales orders and quotes to processing returns and running a point-of-sale terminal.

## What Sales owns

- **Sales orders** — create and manage customer orders
- **Returns** — process customer return requests and refunds
- **Point of sale** — in-person sales terminal for retail or counter operations

## Data flows out of Sales to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Sales revenue |
| Inventory | Stock depletion on confirmed orders |
| Dashboard | Sales metrics |

## Key features

### Sales orders
Create orders from CRM deals or from scratch. Add line items, apply discounts, set delivery dates, and convert to invoices in one click.

### Quotes
Generate professional quotes and send to customers for approval. Convert approved quotes directly to sales orders.

### Returns
Process full or partial returns against original orders. Automatically triggers stock adjustments in Inventory and credit notes in Finance.

### Point of sale
A streamlined POS interface for counter and retail sales. Supports cash, card, and split payments.
