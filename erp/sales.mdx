Sales & POS is where you sell: at the counter, by order, and from quotes. Every sale updates your stock and your books.

## How Sales & POS is organised

Tabs: **Sales Ledger**, **Orders**, **Reservations**, **Invoices**, **Quotes**, **Customers**, **Debtors**, **Returns**, **Registers**.

At the top: **POS Terminal** (the register), **Reserve** (hold stock for a customer), **New Order**, and the Sales settings (the gear).

There is also **Softbooq POS**, a separate app for the counter that keeps selling when the internet drops and catches up afterwards.

---

## Common tasks

### Sell at the register

Open **POS Terminal**, add items, and take payment. A customer is optional: a sale can go to **Walk-in Customer**. You need a customer for larger sales and for anything paid only in part or later.

### Take an order

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Order">
    Add the items. Each line can take a **Line discount %**, and the whole order a **Discount**. Add a delivery address and the customer's reference or PO number if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Order">
    The order and its invoice are created together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfil it from the Orders tab">
    Open the order to move it through **New**, **Confirmed**, **Picking**, **Partially shipped**, **Fulfilled** and **Delivered**. To ship part of it, use **Record shipment** and enter the quantity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Send a quote

Create the quote in **Quotes**. A quote needs a **Valid Until** date before it can be sent. From the quote you can:

- **Mark as Sent**
- **Copy Link**: a link where your customer can accept the quote
- download the quote PDF or a **Proforma**
- **Convert to Invoice** once it is accepted: this makes a draft invoice

Quote statuses are **Draft**, **Sent**, **Accepted**, **Declined** and **Expired**.

### Take a return

Open **Returns → Process Return** (or **Returns / Refund** at the register). Find the sale by receipt number or customer, choose the items and quantities, and for each line whether it goes back into stock.

- Reasons: Defective / Damaged, Wrong Item Received, Customer Changed Mind, Not as Described, Size / Fit Issue, Found Better Price, Other Reason
- Refund by: Original Payment Method, Cash, Card / Terminal, Bank Transfer, or Store Credit

A credit note is issued against the invoice.

### Hold stock for a customer

Use **Reserve**. The reservation appears under **Reservations**, where you **Collect** it when the customer picks it up, or release it.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sale asks for a customer">
    A customer is needed for larger sales and for sales paid only in part or later. Choose one, or create one from the cart.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot cancel an order">
    Orders can be cancelled only before they are fulfilled. After that, take a return.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
