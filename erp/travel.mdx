Travel & Expense is where staff ask to travel, claim what they spent, and get it paid back, and where approvers decide. It is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## How it is organised

- **My Travel Desk**: your own **My Trip Records**, **My Expenses**, **My Reimbursements** and **Receipt Inbox**
- **Organization Hub** for administrators, or **Approvals** for approvers: everything waiting for a decision, with bulk approval
- **Travel Intelligence**: spending and trip analytics
- **Bookings**: flights, stays, cars and eSIMs, for people allowed to book travel

**New Record** at the top opens a **Trip Request** or an **Expense Report**. The gear opens Travel Settings, where the **Policy Engine** holds your travel and expense rules.

---

## Common tasks

### Ask to travel

**New Record → Trip Request**. Choose the kind of trip (business, conference, training, client visit, other), where and when. It goes to whoever approves it, as your approval policies say.

### Claim expenses

**New Record → Expense Report** opens a wizard: **Details**, **Mileage**, **Per Diem**, **Allowances**, **Receipts**, **Review**, then **Submit Report**. You can link it to an approved trip.

On **Receipts**, add each receipt; Softbooq reads it and fills in the amount, marked **Filled in by Softbooq AI from the receipt** for you to check. If a receipt is in another currency you can enter the rate you actually got. Receipts can also arrive in your **Receipt Inbox**: forward them by email or upload them.

### Approve

Approvers see what is waiting in **Organization Hub** or **Approvals**, with how well each claim follows your policy, and can approve several at once. Approved reimbursements are paid from Finance (**Purchases → Reimbursements**), and staff follow theirs in **My Reimbursements**.

### Book travel and buy an eSIM

On **Bookings**, search flights, stays and cars, or choose the eSIM pill to buy a travel data plan for yourself or an employee. See [eSIMs](/integrations/esim).
