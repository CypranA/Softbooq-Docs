The Travel & Expense module manages business travel and employee expense claims — from booking a trip and capturing receipts to submitting an expense report and processing reimbursement through Finance.

## What Travel & Expense owns

- **Trips** — business travel records with destination, dates, and purpose
- **Expense reports** — grouped expense submissions pending approval and reimbursement
- **Receipts** — individual expense items with attached proof of purchase

## Data flows into Travel & Expense from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| HR | Employee travel policies and per diem rates |

## Data flows out of Travel & Expense to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Approved reimbursements |

## Key features

### Trip management
Create a trip record for any business travel. Log associated expenses directly against the trip for easy reporting and reimbursement.

### Expense reports
Group individual receipts into an expense report and submit for manager approval. Track approval status in real time.

### Receipt capture
Upload receipt images directly from mobile. The system extracts amount, date, and merchant details automatically.

### Reimbursement
Approved expense reports are posted to Finance as reimbursement payables, ready to be included in the next payment run.
