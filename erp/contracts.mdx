Contract Management keeps your contracts with customers and suppliers: their terms, signatures, payment milestones and renewals. It is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## How it is organised

A switch at the top shows **Client** or **Supplier** contracts. Below, the contracts themselves, with their e-signatures, milestones and **Contract Templates**. **AI Smart Scan** reads a signed contract you upload and fills in a new record for you to check.

---

## Common tasks

### Add a contract

Choose **Create Contract** and pick how it is signed:

- **Internal (Digital Signature)**: signed in Softbooq.
- **External (Already Signed)**: it was signed elsewhere, so you attach the signed PDF.

Choose the other party, the start and end dates, the value and the **Billing Frequency** (one-off, monthly, quarterly or yearly). Tick **Enable Auto-Renewal** for a contract that renews by its own term. Depending on your approval policies, it may wait for approval before it becomes active.

### Get it signed

Choose **Request Signatures** (or **Request E-Signatures** on the signatures tab), add each signer with their role, and **Send Requests**, or create the signing links to send yourself. Requests expire after 14 days. The signatures tab shows who has signed, and why anyone declined.

### Bill against milestones

On **Payment Milestones**, choose **Add Milestone**. A milestone linked to a project task is completed when the task is done. **Generate Invoice** creates a draft invoice for it in Finance.

### Record a change

**Add New Amendment** records what changed and why. It does not change the contract's dates or value; edit those on the contract itself.

### Renewals and reminders

Contracts set to renew automatically renew by their own term every night, catching up on any term that was missed. You are reminded 30, 7 and 1 day before a contract ends.

### Templates

**Contract Templates** are written in the app, with fields that fill in from the contract.
