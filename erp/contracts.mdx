The Contracts module manages the full lifecycle of customer and supplier contracts — from drafting and e-signature through to milestone tracking, amendments, and renewal alerts.

## What Contracts owns

- **Contracts** — the master contract record with parties, value, and dates
- **Milestones** — payment or delivery milestones linked to contract terms
- **Signatures** — e-signature requests and signed document storage

## Data flows into Contracts from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| CRM | Customer data for contract creation |

## Data flows out of Contracts to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Contract value for revenue recognition |

## Key features

### Contract management
Create contracts linked to CRM customers. Set start and end dates, contract value, payment terms, and renewal conditions. Receive alerts before contracts expire.

### Milestones
Break a contract into milestones with individual due dates and amounts. Milestone completion can trigger automatic invoice generation in Finance.

### E-signatures
Send contracts for electronic signature directly from the platform. Track signature status and store signed documents securely.

### Amendments
Record formal amendments to existing contracts with a complete audit trail of changes.
