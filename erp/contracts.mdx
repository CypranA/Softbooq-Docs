The Contracts module manages the full lifecycle of customer and supplier contracts — from drafting and e-signature through to milestone tracking, amendments, and renewal alerts.

## What Contracts owns

- **Contracts** — the master contract record with parties, value, and dates
- **Milestones** — payment or delivery milestones linked to contract terms
- **Signatures** — e-signature requests and signed document storage

## Data flows into Contracts from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| CRM | Customer data for contract creation |

## Data flows out of Contracts to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Contract value for revenue recognition |

## Common tasks

### Create a contract

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contracts → New Contract" />
  <Step title="Link to a customer">
    Select the customer from your CRM. Their details pre-populate the contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set contract terms">
    Enter start date, end date, contract value, payment terms, and renewal conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the contract document">
    Attach the contract PDF or draft the terms directly in the rich text editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The contract is active and you will receive an alert before it expires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Request an e-signature

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract → click Request Signature" />
  <Step title="Add signatories">
    Enter the email addresses of everyone who needs to sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Each signatory receives an email with a secure link to review and sign the document electronically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track status">
    The contract record shows who has signed and who is outstanding in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a milestone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract → Milestones tab → New Milestone" />
  <Step title="Set the milestone details">
    Enter the milestone name, due date, and the amount due on completion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable auto-invoicing (optional)">
    Toggle **Auto-create invoice** to have Finance automatically generate an invoice when this milestone is marked complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Record an amendment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contract → click Add Amendment" />
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Write a summary of what is being amended and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the contract terms if needed">
    Change any relevant fields (value, dates, terms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The amendment is stored with a timestamp. The full history of changes is visible in the contract's audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Contract management** — Full lifecycle from draft to signed to expired. Renewal alerts before contracts lapse.

**Milestones** — Break contracts into payment or delivery milestones. Optionally auto-generate invoices in Finance on completion.

**E-signatures** — Send for electronic signature directly from the platform. Signed documents stored securely.

**Amendments** — Formal amendment records with a complete, immutable audit trail.
