Human Resources (HR for short) holds your employees and everything about their work: time, leave, pay, hiring and the rules behind them. It is part of Softbooq Pro.

## How HR is organised

HR has five sections along the top:

| Section | What is in it |
|---------|---------------|
| **Me** | Your own time, absence, profile and documents (payslips are under **My documents**) |
| **Team** | **Directory**, Departments and the org chart |
| **Operations** | Analytics, **Attendance**, **Time Approvals**, Billable Report, **Leave**, Team Calendar, **Payroll**, Cost per Employee, Performance, Delegations |
| **Talent** | **Job Openings**, **Candidate Pipeline**, Application Templates |
| **Governance** | Schedules, **Approval Policies**, **Leave Types**, Public Holidays, Onboarding Templates, Offboarding Policies |

HR also keeps positions and job grades, skills, courses and credentials with their renewals, surveys and succession plans.

People without HR admin rights see **Me** first: their own time, leave and documents.

## Common tasks

### Add an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Team → Directory → Add Employee" />
  <Step title="Fill in the essentials">
    Name, email, position, department and team, who they report to, cost center and hourly rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite them to sign in (optional)">
    Tick the invitation and choose their role. An employee record without a sign-in does not use a paid seat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Personal details and salary are added afterwards on the employee's profile.

### Run payroll

<Steps>
  <Step title="Operations → Payroll → New Payroll Run" />
  <Step title="Bring in tax and deductions">
    By default Softbooq does not calculate tax. Your payroll provider works out tax and statutory deductions, and you import the results. Softbooq records the run and posts it to your accounts; use certified payroll software for statutory filing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process Run, then Post to Accounting" />
  <Step title="Send payslips, then Mark paid" />
</Steps>

To leave someone out of a run, use **Leave out of this run** on their line.

### Request and approve leave

An employee chooses **Request Leave**, then **Submit Request**. Whoever approves it, as set in **Approval Policies**, chooses **Approve Request** or **Reject Request**; a rejection needs a reason. Kinds of leave are set in **Governance → Leave Types**, each marked as annual, sick, unpaid, parental or other: sick leave stays private, and unpaid leave reduces pay automatically. A new request opens on annual leave.

### Track time

Everyone can clock in and out, with breaks, from the clock at the top, and see their hours under **Me → My time**. Managers check attendance under **Operations → Attendance** and approve hours under **Time Approvals**. Approved hours are what projects and billing use.

### When someone leaves

Open the employee's profile and the **Lifecycle** tab: choose the exit policy and type, work through the checklist, check what still needs a new owner, then **Finalize Exit & Release Access**. Equipment assigned to them goes back into the asset register.

### Hire

Publish a role in **Talent → Job Openings**; it appears on your careers page. Applicants move through the **Candidate Pipeline**. See [Careers](/portals/careers).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can see salaries and payslips?">
    Salaries, payslips and payroll totals need the sensitive-HR permission. Everyone sees their own payslips under **Me → My documents**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Softbooq file payroll taxes?">
    No. Tax and deductions come from your payroll provider. Softbooq records the run, posts it to your accounts and sends payslips.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
