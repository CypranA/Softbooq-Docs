The HR module manages the complete employee lifecycle — from onboarding a new hire to processing monthly payroll, managing leave requests, and tracking attendance.

## What HR owns

- **Employees** — the master employee record used across the ERP
- **Payroll** — calculate and process monthly salary runs
- **Leave requests** — manage annual leave, sick leave, and other absence types
- **Attendance** — clock-in/clock-out tracking and timesheets
- **Onboarding** — structured onboarding checklists for new hires

## Data flows out of HR to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Payroll for accounting |
| Projects | Resource allocation |
| Travel & Expense | Employee policies |
| Compliance | Employee acknowledgements |
| User Management | Employee accounts |
| Notifications | HR alerts and approvals |
| Audit Logs | Personnel changes |
| Dashboard | Workforce metrics |
| Reports | Workforce data |

## Key features

### Employee profiles
A complete employee record including personal details, role, department, salary, and documents. Referenced by Projects, Travel, Compliance, and Payroll.

### Payroll
Run monthly payroll with automatic deduction calculations. Export payslips as PDF and post payroll entries directly to Finance.

### Leave management
Employees submit leave requests through the self-service portal. Managers approve or reject with a single click. Leave balances update automatically.

### Attendance
Clock-in and clock-out tracking with daily and monthly timesheets. Integrates with payroll for accurate hours-based pay calculations.

### Onboarding
Create structured onboarding checklists assigned to new hires. Track completion and send automated reminders.
