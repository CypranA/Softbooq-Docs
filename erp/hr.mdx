The HR module manages the complete employee lifecycle — from onboarding a new hire to processing monthly payroll, managing leave requests, and tracking attendance.

## What HR owns

- **Employees** — the master employee record used across the ERP
- **Payroll** — calculate and process monthly salary runs
- **Leave requests** — manage annual leave, sick leave, and other absence types
- **Attendance** — clock-in/clock-out tracking and timesheets
- **Onboarding** — structured onboarding checklists for new hires

## Data flows out of HR to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Payroll for accounting |
| Projects | Resource allocation |
| Travel & Expense | Employee policies |
| Compliance | Employee acknowledgements |
| Reports | Workforce data |

## Common tasks

### Add a new employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HR → Employees → New Employee" />
  <Step title="Enter personal details">
    Fill in full name, date of birth, national ID, contact details, and emergency contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set role and department">
    Assign a job title, department, and reporting manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set compensation">
    Enter the salary, pay frequency (monthly, bi-weekly), and start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload documents">
    Attach the signed contract and any required ID documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The employee is now available across Projects, Travel, Compliance, and Payroll.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Run payroll

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HR → Payroll → New Payroll Run" />
  <Step title="Select the period">
    Choose the pay period (e.g. January 2025). The system pulls in all active employees and their salaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review deductions">
    Check automatically calculated tax, pension, and other deductions. Adjust any exceptions manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process">
    Click **Process Payroll**. Payslips are generated as PDFs for each employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post to Finance">
    Click **Post to Finance** to create the payroll journal entry in your accounts automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Approve a leave request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HR → Leave or check your notifications">
    Pending leave requests appear in your notification inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the request">
    Check the dates, leave type, and the employee's remaining balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Click **Approve** or **Reject**. The employee is notified immediately and their leave balance updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Employee profiles** — Complete record including personal details, role, department, salary, and documents. Referenced by Projects, Travel, Compliance, and Payroll.

**Payroll** — Monthly payroll with automatic deduction calculations. Payslips export as PDF and post directly to Finance.

**Leave management** — Employees submit requests via self-service. Managers approve with one click. Balances update automatically.

**Attendance** — Clock-in/clock-out with daily and monthly timesheets. Integrates with payroll for hours-based calculations.

**Onboarding** — Structured checklists assigned to new hires with automated reminders until complete.
