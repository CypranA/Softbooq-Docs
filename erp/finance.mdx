The Finance module is the financial hub of your ERP. Every monetary transaction across all other modules — sales revenue, payroll, vendor bills, project billing, asset depreciation — flows into Finance automatically.

## What Finance owns

- **Invoices** — create, send, and track customer invoices with payment status
- **Expenses** — log and categorise company expenses
- **Vendor bills** — record and manage supplier invoices
- **Journal entries** — manual accounting entries and adjustments
- **Credit notes** — issue credits against existing invoices
- **Payments** — record inbound and outbound payments

## Data flows into Finance from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| CRM | Customer invoices |
| Sales & POS | Sales revenue |
| HR | Payroll |
| Procurement | Vendor bills |
| Projects | Billable time |
| Travel & Expense | Reimbursements |
| Assets | Depreciation |
| Contracts | Contract value |

## Key features

### Invoices
Create professional invoices from quotes or from scratch. Send directly via email, track payment status, and issue credit notes for returns or disputes.

### Expenses
Log expenses against categories and cost centres. Attach receipts and link to projects or departments for accurate reporting.

### Payments
Record customer payments against outstanding invoices. Support for partial payments, overpayments, and multi-currency transactions.

### Journal entries
Manual double-entry bookkeeping for adjustments, opening balances, and corrections.

## Connects to

- [Reports](/erp/reports) — financial data feeds into all financial reports
- [Dashboard](/erp/overview) — financial KPIs surface on the main dashboard
