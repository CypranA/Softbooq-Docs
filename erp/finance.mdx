Finance is where your money is recorded: what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, and the books behind every report. Sales, purchases, payroll and assets from the other modules post here automatically.

## How Finance is organised

<Frame caption="The five sections along the top, the tabs of the open section underneath, and New Invoice on the right.">
  <img src="/images/erp/finance-invoices.jpg" alt="Finance with the five sections along the top and the Sales tabs underneath" />
</Frame>


Finance has five sections along the top. Each has its own tabs underneath.

| Section | Tabs |
|---------|------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview, **Books Health** |
| **Sales** | Invoices, Recurring Invoices, Credit Notes, Billable Time, Collections, Payments |
| **Purchases** | Bills, Expenses, Recurring Expenses, Vendor Credits, Reimbursements, Invoice Inbox, Vendor Bills, Payment Runs |
| **Planning** | Budget, Forecast, Allocation Rules |
| **Accounting** | Journal Entries, General Ledger, A/P Aging, Chart of Accounts, Cost Centers, Dimensions, Accruals & Amortization, Period End Closing, Reconciliation, Bank Feeds, Books Health |

Revenue Recognition, Provisions and Development Costs also appear under Accounting unless advanced accounting is switched off in the Finance settings (the gear).

**Books Health** checks whether your books can be trusted and offers repairs for what it finds. It sits next to your figures under Dashboard, and under Accounting.

<Note>
  Finance needs the Finance permissions. Admins and Managers have them; Employees and Viewers do not see Finance.
</Note>

## Common tasks

### Create and send an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sales → Invoices → New Invoice" />
  <Step title="Fill in the invoice">
    Choose the customer, add the lines, and check the currency, payment terms and status. A new invoice starts as **Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Invoice" />
  <Step title="Send it">
    In the invoice list, choose **Send Email** on the invoice. Emailing a draft marks it **Sent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For invoices that repeat, use **Sales → Recurring Invoices → Create Template**: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, and paused or active.

### Record a payment

Open the invoice and choose **Record Payment**. Enter the amount, the method and the account it was paid into. If the customer has credit with you, the dialog offers **Apply ... of credit**, and you can tick **Email the customer a receipt**.

A part payment leaves the invoice **Partially Paid**; full payment makes it **Paid**. A payment larger than the invoice becomes credit for that customer.

### Bills from suppliers

Supplier bills reach **Purchases → Bills** from the **Invoice Inbox** (forwarded or uploaded) and from purchase orders (**Create Bill** in Procurement). Bills move through the stages **Inbox**, **To approve**, **To pay**, **Paid** (or **Rejected**). **Pay** takes you to **Payment Runs**, where you pay several suppliers at once or export a bank file.

### Log an expense

**Purchases → Expenses → Record Expense**. Pick a category and enter the total on the receipt: the net amount and the VAT are worked out at the right rate, and stay editable. Add a cost center or project if you like, and attach the receipt. Expenses that staff paid themselves and want back are under **Reimbursements**.

### Correct an invoice with a credit note

Open the invoice and choose **Correct with a credit note**, or go to **Sales → Credit Notes → New credit note** and pick the invoice. Write the reason in your own words. You can save it as a draft first, and record whether goods came back.

### Make a journal entry

**Accounting → Journal Entries → New Journal Entry**. Add the lines, and post when debit and credit balance. To undo a posted entry, use **Reverse entry**: it posts the mirror entry, so the history stays complete.

### Bring in your bank and reconcile

Connect your bank in **Settings → Banking → Bank Feeds**, or upload a statement file (OFX, QIF, CAMT.053 or MT940). Transactions appear under **Accounting → Bank Feeds**. To match them to invoices, bills and payments, open **Reports → Recon Workbench** and use **Run Auto-Match**, then confirm or reject each suggested match.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see New Invoice">
    Creating invoices needs the Finance management permission. Ask an Admin to check your role in **User Management → Roles & Permissions**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A report looks wrong">
    Open **Books Health**. It shows what is off in the books and, where it can, offers the repair.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The financial year starts in the wrong month">
    It is set in **Settings → Business → Accounting** and cannot be changed once anything has been posted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
