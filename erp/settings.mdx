Settings is the configuration layer for your entire Softbooq workspace. Everything from your company profile and fiscal year to email providers, payment integrations, and subscription billing is managed here.

## What Settings owns

- **Tenant configuration** — company profile, branding, currency, timezone, fiscal year
- **Integrations** — connected third-party services (email, payments, banks)
- **Billing** — your Softbooq subscription plan and usage

## Key sections

### Company profile
Set your company name, logo, registered address, default currency, and timezone. This information appears on all outgoing invoices, quotes, and emails.

### Email
Connect your email provider to send invoices, quotes, and notifications from your own domain. Supports Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, SMTP, SendGrid, Mailgun, and Postmark.

### Payments
Configure outbound payment providers (Wise, Revolut) for batch payment runs and connect your bank for automated reconciliation.

### Users & Roles
Invite team members, assign roles, and manage permissions. Available roles: Admin, Manager, Employee, Viewer, and Client.

### Security
Configure two-factor authentication requirements, session timeout, and IP allowlists.

### Subscription & Billing
View your current plan, usage metrics, and billing history. Upgrade or downgrade your subscription.
