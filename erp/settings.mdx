Settings holds your workspace's configuration. Everyone sees **My Profile** and **Notifications**; the other tabs need the permission to manage settings (Admins have it).

## The Settings tabs

<Frame caption="Search all settings finds any setting by name. The tabs run down the left.">
  <img src="/images/erp/settings.jpg" alt="The Settings tabs down the left and the search box above them" />
</Frame>


| Tab | What it holds |
|-----|---------------|
| My Profile | Your own details, sign-in security and sessions |
| Notifications | Which alerts reach you |
| Business | **Business Profile**, **Accounting**, **Tax & VAT**, **Locations**, **Public Page**, **Online shop**, **Documents** |
| Legal & Compliance | Your privacy policy, terms and legal notice, legal hold, and support access |
| Softbooq AI | Switch AI on for the workspace, the AI credit policy, usage, and how long conversations are kept |
| Automations | Scheduled alerts: low stock, contracts ending, overdue invoices, and a weekly digest. They only notify you; they never message customers or change data |
| Messaging & Channels | **Email Service** (who sends your email), instant messaging, and **Email Activity** (what was sent and whether it arrived) |
| Email Templates | The wording of the emails Softbooq sends for you |
| Billing & Plans | Your plan, the Admin Portal, Manage Billing, and exporting your accounting records |
| Banking | **My Accounts**, **Receive Payments**, **Send Payments / Payouts**, **Bank Feeds** |
| Integrations | Connected services, grouped: Identity & Directory, Commerce, Accounting, CRM, Project Management, Device Management, Travel, HR |
| API keys | Keys that let your own tools read your data. A key can only read, only what you choose, and never more than you can see yourself |
| Preferences | Workspace defaults (language, date format, work week) that each person can override |
| Recycle Bin | Records deleted in the last 30 days, which you can put back |

### Moved out of Settings

| Looking for | It is now in |
|-------------|--------------|
| Users, roles and permissions | The **User Management** module: User List, Roles & Permissions, Security Policy |
| Security policy (MFA, inactivity lock) | **User Management → Security Policy** |
| Audit log | The **Audit Logs** module (Softbooq Pro) |
| Sales channels | **Reports → Channel Performance → Manage channels** |
| Changing plan or seats | **Billing & Plans → Open Admin Portal** |

## Common tasks

### Set up your business details and logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Business → Business Profile" />
  <Step title="Fill in your company details">
    Your company name, address and registration details appear on your documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Up to 2 MB. It appears on your documents and on your public site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set the start of your financial year

In **Settings → Business → Accounting**, choose **Fiscal Year Start Month**. Set it before you post anything: it defines every period, close and period lock, and it cannot be changed once anything has been posted.

### Set up VAT or sales tax

In **Settings → Business → Tax & VAT**, say whether your business charges VAT/GST, what you can reclaim, and your **Tax codes**.

### Send email from your own domain

Softbooq sends your email from its own service from day one, within a monthly allowance of free emails; after that, emails are held until the next month. To send from your own domain with no limit, open **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service**, choose **Your own domain**, **Brevo** or **Custom SMTP (advanced)**, then use **Test Connection**. **Email Activity** shows every email sent and whether it was delivered, bounced or held.

### Connect payments and your bank

Open **Settings → Banking**. **Receive Payments** connects Stripe so customers can pay online. **Send Payments / Payouts** connects Wise or Revolut for paying suppliers. **Bank Feeds** brings in your bank transactions.

### Require two-factor sign-in

Open **User Management → Security Policy → Two-factor authentication**, choose the **Roles that must use two-factor authentication**, and a **Grace period (days)** to set it up (0 = right away). The same screen has the **Desktop inactivity lock** and **Workspace Access** (only people with an email address on your domain can join).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see most Settings tabs">
    Only My Profile and Notifications are open to everyone. The other tabs need the permission to manage settings; ask an Admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot change the start of the financial year">
    It is locked once anything has been posted, because moving it would re-slice periods that are already closed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emails are not going out">
    Open **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Activity**. If the monthly free emails are used up, emails are held until next month; connect your own email provider and send them again from there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I deleted something by mistake">
    Open **Settings → Recycle Bin**. Records deleted in the last 30 days can be put back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
