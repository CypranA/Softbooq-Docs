Settings is the configuration layer for your entire Softbooq workspace. Everything from your company profile and fiscal year to email providers, payment integrations, and subscription billing is managed here.

## What Settings owns

- **Tenant configuration** — company profile, branding, currency, timezone, fiscal year
- **Integrations** — connected third-party services (email, payments, banks)
- **Billing** — your Softbooq subscription plan and usage

---

## Common tasks

### Complete your company profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Company Profile" />
  <Step title="Fill in your company details">
    Enter company name, registered address, VAT/tax number, default currency, and timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Upload your company logo. It will appear on all invoices, quotes, and emails sent from Softbooq.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your fiscal year">
    Choose your accounting period start month. This is required before you can run any financial reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

### Connect your email provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Email → Connect Provider" />
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Select from Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, SMTP, SendGrid, Mailgun, or Postmark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Follow the provider-specific flow (OAuth for Microsoft/Google, API key for SendGrid/Mailgun/Postmark, credentials for SMTP).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Click **Send Test** to verify the connection is working before going live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Users & Roles → Invite User" />
  <Step title="Enter their email address" />
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Choose: Admin, Manager, Employee, Viewer, or Client. See [Quickstart](/quickstart) for a role breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    They receive an email to set their password and access the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enable two-factor authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Security" />
  <Step title="Toggle Require 2FA">
    When enabled, all users in your workspace must set up 2FA on their next login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Existing sessions are not affected immediately — users are prompted at next login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Subscription & Billing" />
  <Step title="View your current plan">
    See your active plan, usage metrics (users, storage, modules), and next billing date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade or downgrade">
    Click **Change Plan** to switch tiers. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key sections

**Company profile** — Name, logo, address, currency, timezone. Appears on all outgoing documents.

**Email** — Connect your email provider. Supports Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, SMTP, SendGrid, Mailgun, and Postmark.

**Payments** — Configure Wise or Revolut for outbound payment runs and bank reconciliation.

**Users & Roles** — Invite team members, assign roles, and manage permissions.

**Security** — Two-factor authentication requirements, session timeout, and IP allowlists.

**Subscription & Billing** — View your plan, usage, and billing history. Upgrade or downgrade at any time.
