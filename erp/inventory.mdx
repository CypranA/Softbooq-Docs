Products & Services holds what you sell and the stock you keep: in each store and, if you use them, in storage areas and bins. Sales, purchases and production all move stock here.

## How it is organised

Tabs: **Products**, **Service packages**, **Transfers**, **Returns**, **Loss history** and **Reorder radar**. With **Storage areas and bins** switched on in **Inventory Settings** (the gear), **Stock levels** and **Storage areas** appear too.

Your stores and other locations, a warehouse site among them, are set up in **Settings → Business → Locations**. A storage area is an area or shelf inside one location, with its bins; stock totals and sales stay per location.

---

## Common tasks

### Add a product

Choose **Add Product**, or **Scan Label** to read a product label into a new product. Give it a name and price; for stock you can enter the opening quantity and where it is kept. **Low Stock Alert at** sets the level at which it shows as low. A product can have options such as sizes and colours, each with its own stock, SKU and barcode.

To import many at once, upload a CSV with the columns **name** and **price** (both required), and optionally sku, category, stock and cost.

### Change stock

Stock is not typed over. It changes when goods are received, sold, returned, produced or moved, and you correct it with:

- **Report Loss**, for stock that is damaged, stolen, expired or wrongly recorded. It is listed under **Loss history**.
- **Count stock** on a phone: scan a barcode and enter what is on the shelf. See [Softbooq on your phone](/erp/phone).
- With storage areas on, **Adjust** on a bin under **Stock levels**.

### Move stock between stores

Choose **New Transfer**, pick where it comes from and where it goes, add the items, and **Create Transfer Request**. The stock moves when the transfer is created; after that its status (Pending, In Transit, Completed) records where the goods are.

### Know when to reorder

**Reorder radar** shows, for each product, how fast it sells, how many days of stock are left, how urgent a reorder is, and how much to order. It is worked out from your own sales and lead times; choose **Explain**, then **Generate explanation**, for Jacinta to explain a line in plain words.

### Track expiry dates

Tick **Track expiry dates** on a product, and its batch number and expiry are asked for when stock is received.

---

## How stock is valued

Choose the costing method in **Inventory Settings**: FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average or Standard. Changing it needs a reason, and past entries are not restated. A product's cost cannot be edited while stock is held: use **Revalue stock**, which posts the change to your books.
