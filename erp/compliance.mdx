Compliance holds your company policies and records who has read and acknowledged each one. It is part of Softbooq Pro.

## How it is organised

Tabs: **Policies**, **Acknowledgments** and **Audit log**. Everyone can read policies and acknowledge them; creating and changing them, and seeing who has acknowledged, needs the permission to manage settings.

---

## Common tasks

### Add a policy

Choose **New Policy** to open **Create Policy Document**, or **From template** to start from one of the ready-made policies. Give it a title, description, category and version, the date it takes effect and, if it has one, the date it is retired, and attach the document or link to it. A new policy is saved as a draft.

### Publish it

Open the draft and choose **Activate**. Every person in the workspace can then see it and **Acknowledge** it. A policy that is no longer in force can be set to **Archive**.

### Bring out a new version

Edit the policy, change its version and describe what changed. Everyone is asked to acknowledge the new version.

### See who has acknowledged

**Acknowledgments** shows, for each policy, how many people have acknowledged it and how many have not. **Remind Pending Users** sends a reminder to everyone who has not. **Export as CSV** or PDF gives you the record.

---

## Policies from Confluence

If you link Confluence spaces to policy categories, their published pages appear here as policies too. See [Confluence](/integrations/confluence).
