The Compliance module ensures your workforce has read, understood, and formally acknowledged your company's policies — with a full audit trail of who acknowledged what and when.

## What Compliance owns

- **Policies** — company policy documents distributed to employees
- **Acknowledgements** — employee sign-off records per policy

## Data flows into Compliance from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| HR | Employee records for policy assignment |

## Key features

### Policy management
Upload and publish policy documents (PDF or rich text). Assign policies to all employees or specific departments and roles.

### Acknowledgement tracking
Employees receive a notification to read and acknowledge each assigned policy. Track completion rates by department and send reminders to outstanding employees.

### Audit trail
Every acknowledgement is timestamped and immutable — providing a defensible record for audits, regulatory inspections, or HR disputes.

### Policy versioning
When a policy is updated, all affected employees are required to re-acknowledge the new version. Previous acknowledgements are retained for historical reference.
