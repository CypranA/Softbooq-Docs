The Compliance module ensures your workforce has read, understood, and formally acknowledged your company's policies — with a full audit trail of who acknowledged what and when.

## What Compliance owns

- **Policies** — company policy documents distributed to employees
- **Acknowledgements** — employee sign-off records per policy

## Data flows into Compliance from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| HR | Employee records for policy assignment |

---

## Common tasks

### Create and publish a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance → Policies → New Policy" />
  <Step title="Name the policy">
    Give it a clear name (e.g. Code of Conduct, Remote Work Policy, Data Protection Policy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the policy content">
    Write the policy in the rich text editor or upload a PDF document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to employees">
    Choose **All employees** or select specific departments or roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. Each assigned employee receives a notification to read and acknowledge the policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Track acknowledgements

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance → Policies → select the policy" />
  <Step title="View the acknowledgement status">
    The policy record shows a list of all assigned employees with their status: Acknowledged, Pending, or Overdue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send reminders">
    Select all pending employees and click **Send Reminder** to nudge them by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Update a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the existing policy → click Edit" />
  <Step title="Make your changes" />
  <Step title="Save as new version">
    Click **Publish New Version**. All previously assigned employees are required to re-acknowledge the updated policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Previous acknowledgements are retained">
    The audit trail preserves all historical acknowledgements against the version they applied to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Key features

**Policy management** — Upload PDFs or write policies in rich text. Assign to all staff or specific departments.

**Acknowledgement tracking** — Real-time completion rates by department. Send reminders to outstanding employees in one click.

**Audit trail** — Every acknowledgement is timestamped and immutable — defensible in audits, regulatory inspections, or HR disputes.

**Policy versioning** — Updates require re-acknowledgement. Full history of every version and who signed each one.
