When Softbooq emails something for you, it shows whether it arrived. On an **invoice** or **quote** you see its latest email in one line. On a **customer**, you see emails to their address that did not get through. Every email is listed in **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Activity**.

## What the line can say

| It says | What happened |
|---------|---------------|
| Delivered | The receiving server accepted it, on the date shown |
| Waiting to be sent | It is in the queue and goes out within a few minutes |
| Delayed | The address could not take it yet; delivery is being retried |
| Did not arrive: the address does not accept email | It bounced; the address is wrong or closed |
| Did not arrive: refused | The receiving server blocked it or treated it as spam |
| Not sent: held by this month's free email limit | Your monthly free emails are used up (see below) |
| Not sent: no email service is set up, or your email service refused the sign-in | Check your email settings |
| Sent, and whether it arrived shows once the provider reports it | Delivery reports come in after sending |
| Sent through your own email account | Softbooq cannot see delivery for your own mailbox; a bounce notice arrives in that mailbox |

## Fixing it

The fix is shown next to the problem:

- **Correct the customer's email**, then send it again from the invoice or quote.
- **Send again** from Email Activity, for example for emails that were held.
- Open your email settings when no service is set up or your provider refused the sign-in.

To understand what happened, choose **Ask Jacinta** next to it.

## Held emails

Softbooq's own email service includes a set number of free emails each month. When they are used up, further invoices, reminders and notifications are **held**, not lost, until the next month. Customer sign-in codes still go out. To send without a limit, connect your own email provider in **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service**, then use **Send again** in Email Activity.
