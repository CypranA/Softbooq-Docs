Messages is where you talk to people: your colleagues in **Team**, and your customers in **Customers**. One entry in the sidebar, one icon at the top of every page, and one count on both: the team messages you have not read, plus the customer conversations still waiting for an answer.

## How it is organised

Open **Messages** in the sidebar, or the messages icon at the top of every page. The two sections sit side by side at the top, each with its own count:

- **Team**: direct messages and channels with your colleagues. See [Team chat](/erp/teamchat).
- **Customers**: every conversation with a customer, from their account on your shop, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and comments on your posts. The red count is how many are still open.

You see the sections your role allows. Team needs the permission to use team chat; Customers needs access to Sales & POS, the same as before. Someone who has only one of them sees that one, without the bar. A cashier without sales access, for example, sees their team chat and nothing about customers, and their count is their team messages alone.

**Connect apps**, at the top right, shows which messaging apps are connected (Facebook and Instagram, WhatsApp, Twilio, Slack) and takes you to where you connect them.

## Common tasks

### Answer a customer

Open **Customers** and pick a conversation. On a computer it opens beside the list; on a phone it takes the whole screen, and the back arrow returns to the list. Choose **Answer this** so your colleagues know you have it, write your reply and send it. Tick **Note for the team only** to leave a note the customer does not see.

Everything you could do before is still there: filter by **Open** or **All** and by who is answering, link the person to a customer, put in an invoice, quote, order or product, insert a saved reply, send an approved WhatsApp template after 24 hours, and mark the conversation done.

### Format a message

In team chat, wrap words the way WhatsApp does: `*bold*`, `_italic_`, `~strikethrough~` and a backtick on each side for monospace. Bold and italic can go together, as in `*_this_*`. The message is saved exactly as you typed it.

### Write on a phone

The box grows as you type, up to five lines, and then scrolls inside. While you type, the conversation stays above the keyboard on its newest message, and the bar at the bottom of the screen steps aside. **+** adds a photo or file, a picture from the camera, or a record.

### Where conversations with customers went

Conversations with customers used to be a tab in **Sales & POS**. They are in **Messages → Customers** now, and Sales & POS no longer has that tab. Every link to a conversation from before (an alert, an email, a bookmark) still opens it in **Messages → Customers**.
