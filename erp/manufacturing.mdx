The Manufacturing module manages the conversion of raw materials into finished goods — tracking production runs, bills of materials, and feeding completed output back into Inventory.

## What Manufacturing owns

- **Production runs** — individual manufacturing orders with status and output tracking
- **Bills of materials (BOM)** — the recipe of components required to produce each finished product

## Data flows into Manufacturing from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Inventory | Raw materials consumed in production |

## Data flows out of Manufacturing to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Inventory | Finished goods added to stock |

## Key features

### Bills of materials
Define the components, quantities, and sub-assemblies required to manufacture each product. BOMs are versioned so you can track changes over time.

### Production runs
Create a production run against a BOM. The system reserves raw materials from Inventory, tracks work-in-progress, and posts finished goods back to Inventory on completion.

### Production status
Track production runs through stages: planned, in progress, quality check, and completed.

### Yield tracking
Record actual output against expected output to identify waste and inefficiencies in the production process.
