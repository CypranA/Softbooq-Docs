Manufacturing turns materials into finished goods. A bill of materials (BOM) says what goes into one unit, a production run makes a quantity of it, and every run produces a lot you can trace and inspect. It is part of Softbooq Pro.

## How Manufacturing is organised

Tabs: **BOM Library**, **Production Runs**, **Lots & Traceability**, **Quality Control** and **Work centres**.

---

## Common tasks

### Write a bill of materials

Choose **New BOM**, pick the product it makes, and add the components needed for **one** unit. **Add overhead** for costs that are not materials. **Cost to make one** shows the total, and becomes the product's cost when you save.

How the product is made, step by step, is its routing: **Define routing** from the product's row, with each step at a work centre and its setup and run minutes. Work centres carry an hourly cost, which becomes the labour in the run's cost.

A BOM that is no longer used can be set aside with **Discontinue BOM**, and restored later.

### Make a production run

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Run">
    Choose the BOM and the quantity. The form shows, for each component, what you need, what you have and any shortage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as Draft or Save as Ready">
    A draft can be finished later; **Mark Ready** when it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute">
    Enter the actual quantity produced and any scrap, with its reason (Defective, Damaged, Burnt, Contaminated, Other). In one step the components are taken from stock, the finished goods are added as a lot, and the run is posted to your books at materials plus labour and overhead. This cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing is reserved before **Execute**: the stock check runs then. **Cancel Run** stops a run before it is executed, and leaves stock untouched. **Print Production Order** gives you a paper order with a sign-off.

### Check quality

A lot starts in **Quarantine**. Choose **New Inspection** to record the result (Pass, Fail or **Conditional**) and what happens next: release it, **Rework**, scrap it, or keep it in quarantine. Rejected units are written off. Inspections happen after the run; they do not hold it up.

### Trace a lot

**Lots & Traceability** shows each lot, the components that went into it, and its inspections.

---

## Results

Each run shows its yield, its cost per unit and how far it came in over or under budget. **Reports → Production Overview** brings them together.
