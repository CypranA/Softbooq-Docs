The Scheduling module manages bookable resources — rooms, equipment, staff time slots — and allows customers or internal teams to make reservations against available slots.

## What Scheduling owns

- **Resources** — bookable assets or people with defined availability
- **Bookings** — confirmed reservations against a resource's schedule

---

## Common tasks

### Add a bookable resource

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scheduling → Resources → New Resource" />
  <Step title="Name the resource">
    Give it a clear name (e.g. Meeting Room A, Service Bay 2, John Smith – Consultant).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set availability">
    Define the days and hours this resource is available (e.g. Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set buffer time (optional)">
    Add a buffer between bookings to allow for setup or travel (e.g. 15 minutes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set booking rules">
    Choose the minimum and maximum booking duration and how far in advance bookings can be made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The resource is now bookable by your team and, if enabled, by clients via the client portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Create a booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Scheduling → Bookings → New Booking" />
  <Step title="Select the resource" />
  <Step title="Choose date and time">
    The calendar shows available slots in real time. Unavailable times are greyed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a customer or employee (optional)">
    Attach the booking to a CRM customer or HR employee for context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The booking is confirmed. The resource calendar updates immediately and a confirmation notification is sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Block time on a resource

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the resource calendar" />
  <Step title="Click on the time slot you want to block" />
  <Step title="Select Block Time">
    Enter a reason (e.g. Maintenance, Out of Office, Reserved).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The slot is blocked and cannot be booked by anyone until the block is removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Key features

**Resource management** — Define availability windows, buffer times, and booking rules per resource.

**Bookings** — Instant confirmation. Resource calendar updates in real time. Accessible from the client portal if enabled.

**Availability calendar** — Day, week, and month views. Booked, available, and blocked slots clearly visible.

**Conflict prevention** — Double-bookings are blocked automatically. Next available slot is suggested.
