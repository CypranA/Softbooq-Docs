Resource Scheduling is one calendar of everything you book: people, rooms, equipment, tables, courts, vehicles and more. Customers can book online too.

## How it is organised

One calendar, with the views **Week**, **Day**, **Month** and **Agenda**, your resources down the side, and **Insights** for bookings, no-shows, hours, revenue and how full each resource is. Click a resource to show only its bookings.

---

## Common tasks

### Add a resource

Choose **Add Resource** and pick its type (Employee, Room, Equipment, Staff, Space, Asset, Table, Vehicle, Chair, Desk, Court, Lane, Station, Booth, Bay, or your own). Set its working hours and its capacity, how many bookings it can take at once, for a class or a court.

- **Linked employee**: link a person, and their approved leave blocks bookings automatically.
- **Available for online booking**: customers can pick this resource when booking a service on your online store.

### Make a booking

Drag across a time on the calendar to open **New Booking**. Choose the resource, the service and customer if there is one, and the status (Confirmed, Pending, Tentative or Cancelled). Tick **Private** to hide the details from colleagues; they see the time as busy.

To repeat it, choose **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Monthly**, and when it stops. Dates that clash, or fall on approved leave, are skipped. When you remove a repeated booking you choose **Remove this occurrence** or **Remove this & future**.

Softbooq will not let a resource be booked over its capacity.

### Afterwards

Mark the booking **Attended** or **No-show**. If it has a service, **Bill this booking** creates the sale and its invoice. A cancelled booking stays on record as Cancelled.

### Sync your own calendar

Under **My Calendars**, you will be able to connect Google or Outlook and see your bookings in your own calendar.
