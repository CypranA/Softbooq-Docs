---
title: Scheduling
description: Resource bookings and availability schedules
---

The Scheduling module manages bookable resources — rooms, equipment, staff time slots — and allows customers or internal teams to make reservations against available slots.

## What Scheduling owns

- **Resources** — bookable assets or people with defined availability
- **Bookings** — confirmed reservations against a resource's schedule

## Data flows out of Scheduling to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| HR | Resource scheduling data |

## Key features

### Resource management
Define bookable resources — meeting rooms, equipment, service bays, consultants — and set their availability windows, buffer times, and booking rules.

### Bookings
Internal staff or customers (via the client portal) can book available slots. Bookings are confirmed instantly and the resource calendar updates in real time.

### Availability calendar
A visual calendar showing booked, available, and blocked time slots per resource. Supports day, week, and month views.

### Conflict prevention
The system prevents double-bookings automatically. If a slot is taken, the next available slot is suggested.
