The Maintenance module manages the upkeep of your physical assets — logging maintenance activities, handling repair requests, and ordering parts through Procurement when needed.

## What Maintenance owns

- **Maintenance logs** — records of completed maintenance activities per asset
- **Maintenance requests** — reported issues and repair requests from staff

## Data flows into Maintenance from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Assets | Asset records for maintenance tracking |

## Data flows out of Maintenance to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Procurement | Parts orders |

## Key features

### Maintenance requests
Staff submit maintenance requests for faulty or damaged equipment. Requests are assigned to technicians and tracked through to resolution.

### Maintenance logs
Every maintenance activity is logged against the asset record — date, technician, work performed, parts used, and cost.

### Preventive maintenance
Schedule recurring maintenance tasks for assets based on time intervals or usage thresholds.

### Parts ordering
When a repair requires parts not in stock, raise a parts order directly to Procurement from within the maintenance request.
