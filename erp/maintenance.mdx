Maintenance keeps your equipment and premises running: anyone can ask for something to be fixed, a manager turns it into a work order, and the work, parts and costs are recorded. It is part of Softbooq Pro.

## How it is organised

Tabs: **Dashboard**, **Work orders**, **Technicians**, **Schedule** and **Requests**. The Dashboard shows what is scheduled, in progress, completed, backlogged and overdue, and what maintenance cost this month and this year.

---

## Common tasks

### Ask for a repair

Choose **Request Maintenance**, pick the asset (or **No asset (location / one-off)**), and describe the problem. The request waits under **Requests**, where a manager approves it, which creates a work order, or rejects it with a reason.

### Work a work order

A work order has a type (Routine, Repair, Inspection, Upgrade, Checkup), a technician, a vendor if a contractor does it, labour hours and cost, and the parts used, taken from your stock or entered by hand. Its status is **Scheduled**, **In Progress**, **Completed** or **Deferred**.

- **Create PO** raises a draft purchase order for what the work needs.
- When the work is completed, the parts used are deducted from stock, and the asset's next service date moves on.
- **Bill contractor** raises the vendor's bill for a completed work order.

### Plan preventive maintenance

On an asset, set its **Maintenance Interval** in months or days. The **Schedule** tab then lists assets that are due: create a work order for one, or **Generate work orders** for all of them at once.

### Manage technicians

Add, edit and remove the people who do the work on **Technicians**.
