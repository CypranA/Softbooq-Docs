The Maintenance module manages the upkeep of your physical assets — logging maintenance activities, handling repair requests, and ordering parts through Procurement when needed.

## What Maintenance owns

- **Maintenance logs** — records of completed maintenance activities per asset
- **Maintenance requests** — reported issues and repair requests from staff

## Data flows into Maintenance from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Assets | Asset records for maintenance tracking |

## Data flows out of Maintenance to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Procurement | Parts orders |

---

## Common tasks

### Submit a maintenance request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Maintenance → Requests → New Request" />
  <Step title="Select the asset">
    Choose the asset that needs attention from the Assets register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">
    Write a clear description of the fault or required work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set priority">
    Choose a priority level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    The request is queued and a notification is sent to your maintenance team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Assign and complete a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Maintenance → Requests and find the request" />
  <Step title="Assign to a technician">
    Select the technician responsible for this repair. They will be notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order parts if needed">
    If parts are required, click **Order Parts** to raise a Procurement request directly from the maintenance record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log the work done">
    Once completed, click **Mark Complete** and enter what was done, parts used, time spent, and cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    A maintenance log entry is created against the asset record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Schedule preventive maintenance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an asset record → Maintenance tab → New Schedule" />
  <Step title="Set the recurrence">
    Choose how often maintenance should occur (e.g. every 3 months, every 500 hours of use).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a default technician (optional)" />
  <Step title="Save">
    Maintenance requests are automatically created on schedule and appear in the maintenance queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Maintenance requests** — Staff submit requests for faulty equipment. Assigned to technicians and tracked through to resolution.

**Maintenance logs** — Every activity logged per asset: date, technician, work performed, parts used, and cost.

**Preventive maintenance** — Recurring schedules based on time intervals or usage thresholds.

**Parts ordering** — Raise a Procurement parts order directly from within a maintenance request.
