Procurement is how you buy: from a request inside your team, to a purchase order, to the goods arriving and the supplier's bill. Paying suppliers happens in Finance, under **Payment Runs**.

## How Procurement is organised

Tabs: **Purchase Orders**, **Suppliers**, **Vendor Credits**, **Requisitions**, **RFQs**, **Recurring POs**, **PO Templates**, **Reports** and **Portal Links**. The Suppliers tab has a **Supplier Scorecard** with on-time delivery and lead times.

---

## Common tasks

### Add a supplier

Choose **Add Supplier**: the business, a contact person, email and phone, address, category, VAT or tax ID and payment terms. For each supplier you can keep what they sell you, with their price, reference, lead time and minimum quantity, and purchase orders fill in from it.

### Ask for something to be bought

Choose **New Requisition**: the department, when it is needed, the items with an estimated price, and why. Who approves it is set in **Human Resources → Governance → Approval Policies**, and the person who raised it can never approve it themselves.

### Get quotes from suppliers

Choose **New RFQ** and add the suppliers. Enter each supplier's quoted total as it comes in, then **Create PO** for the one you choose.

### Raise a purchase order

Choose **New Purchase Order** (or **From a document**, to read a supplier's quote into a new order). Add the supplier and the lines, and, if you like, the project and cost center it is for. When you save, the order is **Sent**, or **Pending Approval** when an approval policy applies to it. You can email it to the supplier from the order.

### Receive the goods

Open the order and choose **Receive Goods**. For each line, choose whether it goes into stock or is used in the business as an expense, and add batch and expiry details or landed costs (freight, customs, handling) if you have them. Then **Confirm Receipt**.

### Record the supplier's bill

From the order, choose **Create Bill**. If the bill does not match what was ordered and received, Softbooq stops it so you can approve it anyway, ask for a correction or reject it. **Add a bill** sends a supplier bill you received to the Invoice Inbox in Finance.

### Let suppliers see their orders

From a supplier's menu, **Share Portal** creates a time-limited link where the supplier sees their orders and quotes requests, acknowledges an order and posts delivery updates. Active links are listed under **Portal Links**.
