**Sales → Deliveries** is the list of orders your own riders take to customers. On a phone, the Sales screen shows how many deliveries are waiting for you; tap it to see where to go. The list updates as riders work, so the desk and the riders see the same thing.

## How an order gets on the list

- **Shop orders** delivered to one of your [delivery areas](/portals/shop-delivery-payments) appear on their own, once any delivery fee you confirm has been approved.
- **Any other order**: open it in **Sales → Orders** and choose **Add to deliveries**. This works for an order that is not cancelled, not a collection in store, and whose delivery fee is agreed.

Changes to the address, landmark or phone made before the order goes out carry over to the delivery.

## Who does what

- **Whoever runs deliveries** chooses the rider and can send a delivery out again. That needs permission to manage sales or to handle online orders.
- **Anyone who can see sales** sees every delivery, and can tick **Only mine** to see their own.
- **The rider** chosen for a delivery can move it along without permission to manage sales. They reach the list through Sales, so their role needs to see sales, as the standard Employee role does. Any active member of your workspace can be chosen as a rider.

## A delivery from start to finish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a rider">
    The rider is told at once, with a notification that opens the delivery. Until then it shows "No rider yet".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Out for delivery">
    The rider chooses **Out for delivery** when they leave. The card shows the address and landmark, a button to call the customer, and what to collect at the door: "Collect" and the amount, or "Nothing to collect at the door."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the money, for pay on delivery">
    The rider chooses **Collected**, enters the **Amount received**, says **How they paid** (**Cash**, **Mobile money** or **Bank transfer**), and chooses **Record payment**. The payment is recorded on the order's invoice. It cannot be more than what is still owed, and a second tap does not record it twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivered">
    The rider chooses **Delivered** and gives proof: **Who received it?**, **Take a photo**, or both. **Mark as delivered** finishes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When it could not be delivered

The rider chooses **Couldn't deliver** and says why: "Nobody was there", "Could not reach them by phone", "Could not find the address", "The customer refused it", or in their own words. The delivery shows **Not delivered**, and nothing is posted to your books.

Whoever runs deliveries chooses **Send out again** to put it back on the list. The card then shows which attempt it is.

## What Delivered does in your books

Marking a delivery **Delivered** ships the order in the same step. Everything on the order not yet shipped is recorded as shipped, the stock goes down, and the sale and its cost of goods are booked. The order then reads Delivered, and so does the customer's order page.

Going out for delivery changes nothing in your books. A delivery marked Delivered cannot be changed afterwards.

A shop order paid through its invoice, by transfer or on delivery, completes once its invoice is fully paid.

## The list

The filters are **To deliver**, **Out for delivery** and **Done**. **Done** shows deliveries finished in the last 14 days, delivered or not.
