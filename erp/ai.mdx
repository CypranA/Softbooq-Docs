Jacinta is Softbooq AI's assistant, built directly into Softbooq. It reads your live business data — invoices, contracts, employees, projects, expenses, and more — and answers questions in plain language. It knows which module you are in and surfaces relevant suggestions before you even type.

<Note>
  Jacinta has two modes. **Fast** finds your records and answers your totals, and is free. **Advanced** filters, compares and prepares work for you, and usually costs 1 credit per message; one message never costs more than 10 credits (3 during a trial). See [AI Credits](/billing/ai-credits).
</Note>

## What Jacinta can do

- Answer questions about your financials, team, pipeline, and operations
- Summarise data across any module (overdue invoices, who is on leave, top clients by revenue)
- Help you understand policies, explain records, and surface patterns in your data
- Cite exactly which records it used to form its answer, so you can verify the source
- Prepare work for you to check, such as a draft invoice or expense, which you open and confirm
- Carry out bulk actions, such as approving several expenses, only after you confirm them on a card

<Note>
  AI must be switched on for your workspace (**Settings → Softbooq AI**) and your role must have the permission **Use the AI assistant**. If you cannot see Jacinta, ask your Admin to check both.
</Note>

## Opening the assistant

Click **Jacinta** (the star) in the sidebar to open the panel. On a phone she is in the top bar. It slides up from the bottom of the screen on mobile and appears as a floating panel on the left side of the screen on desktop.

The panel stays on top of your current module — you can read an answer and immediately act on it without navigating away.

---

## Suggestion chips

When you open a new chat, Jacinta shows suggestion chips tailored to the module you are currently in:

| Module | Suggestions |
|--------|-------------|
| Finance | Cash Flow summary, Overdue invoices, Tax liabilities |
| HR | Recruitment pipeline update, Who is on leave this week, Travel expense policy |
| CRM | Top 5 clients by revenue, High-value lead status, Clients needing follow-up |
| Travel & Expense | Reconcile travel receipts, Upcoming approved itineraries |
| All other modules | Spending trends across modules, Active users in the system |

Click any chip to send that question instantly — no typing required.

---

## Source citations

When Jacinta answers a question using your data, a **Based on your data** label appears below the response. Click it to expand the list of source records the answer was drawn from — invoices, contracts, employees, projects, vendor bills, or expenses.

This lets you verify the answer against the underlying records, or click through to investigate further.

---

## Starting a new chat

Click **New chat** in the panel header to start a fresh conversation. Your earlier chats stay in **Chat history**, where you can reopen them.

## Common questions to ask

- *"Summarise our cash flow for the current month"*

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The AI button is not visible in the sidebar">
    AI must be switched on for your workspace. Go to **Settings → Softbooq AI** and confirm it is on. Your role must also have the permission **Use the AI assistant** (User Management → Roles & Permissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AI is not responding or shows a loading indicator for a long time">
    Jacinta reads your live data and answers in real time, so a complex question can take a little while. If nothing arrives after a minute, close the panel and try again. If the issue persists, check your internet connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The answer does not match my data">
    Expand the **Based on your data** source citations below the response to see which records were used. If the answer references outdated or incorrect records, the underlying data in the relevant module may need to be updated. The AI answers based on what is in your system at the time of the question.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I sent a message and nothing happened">
    There is a 2-second cooldown between messages to prevent duplicate sends. Wait a moment and try again. If the input field appears frozen, close and reopen the panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Jacinta make changes to my data?">
    Yes, with you in control. She prepares drafts, such as an invoice, expense or purchase order, that you open and check before saving. Bulk actions, such as approving expenses or sending invoices, only run after you confirm them. During a trial she answers questions but does not take actions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data sent to a third party?">
    To answer, the relevant text is sent to our AI sub-processors (Anthropic and Google) under contracts that prohibit training on it. See the Softbooq privacy policy at softbooq.com/legal/privacy for full details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI remember previous conversations?">
    Your chats are kept and can be reopened from **Chat history**. An Admin chooses how long they are kept in **Settings → Softbooq AI → Conversation retention** (24 months unless changed). Use **New chat** for a clean slate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can all users use Jacinta?">
    Access is controlled by two settings: AI switched on for the workspace in **Settings → Softbooq AI**, and the role permission **Use the AI assistant** in **User Management → Roles & Permissions**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does Jacinta cost?">
    Fast is free. Advanced usually costs 1 credit per message and never more than 10. Packs start at €5 for 10 credits. Full pricing on [AI Credits](/billing/ai-credits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a standalone AI app?">
    Yes, at ai.softbooq.com. Same assistant, same wallet, full-page interface. See the [AI App](/platform/ai) page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
