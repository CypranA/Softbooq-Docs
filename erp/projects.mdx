Projects is where work is planned and tracked: the tasks, who does them, the time spent, what it costs and what you bill. It is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## How Projects is organised

Two tabs at the top: **Projects** and **Tasks**. In list view, **Budget across projects** shows what is spent, committed and left for every project, and which are over budget.

Inside a project: **Overview**, **Tasks**, **Timeline**, **Time**, **Financials**, **Billing** and **Team**.

---

## Common tasks

### Create a project

Choose **New Project**. In **Create New Project** give it a name and code, a budget, the client (or keep it internal), the priority, a start date, the status it starts in and, if you like, a cost center.

### Plan the work

On **Tasks**, the board has the columns **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Review** and **Done**. A task can depend on another, repeat, carry comments and attachments, and show its estimated against logged hours. **Timeline** shows the plan as a chart over time. **Overview** holds the project's **Milestones** and **Risks**.

### Add people

On **Team**, choose **Add Member**. Team members' bookings in Resource Scheduling show there too, so clashes are visible.

### Log time

Time is logged in **Human Resources**, on the clock or as a time block with the project, task and whether it is billable. A project's **Time** tab shows it; figures count only approved time.

### Watch the budget

**Financials** shows how much of the budget is used and what is left, the spending by category, and the invoices, purchase orders, bills and expenses behind it. Over 80% of the budget, a warning appears. Changes to an agreed budget go through the **Approved baseline** and **Change requests**.

### Bill the client

**Billing** sets payment milestones, each with a payment link for the client. To bill hours, create an invoice in Finance and choose **Import Time**: it lists approved hours not yet invoiced. The rate is the one agreed for the work, otherwise the project's billing rate.
