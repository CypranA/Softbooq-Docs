The Projects module gives your team a structured way to plan, execute, and bill client work — with full integration into Finance for billable time and Procurement for project-specific purchases.

## What Projects owns

- **Projects** — the top-level project record with client, status, and budget
- **Tasks** — individual work items assigned to team members
- **Time entries** — logged hours against tasks and projects
- **Budgets** — planned vs actual cost tracking per project

## Data flows out of Projects to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Billable time for invoicing |
| Procurement | Project purchase orders |
| Notifications | Task alerts |
| Reports | Project data |

## Key features

### Project management
Create projects linked to CRM customers. Set budgets, assign team members sourced from HR, and track progress through configurable stages.

### Task management
Break projects into tasks with assignees, due dates, and priority levels. Kanban and list views available.

### Time tracking
Team members log time directly against tasks. Time entries feed into Finance for client billing and into Reports for utilisation analysis.

### Budget tracking
Set a project budget and track actual spend in real time. Alerts when a project approaches its budget threshold.
