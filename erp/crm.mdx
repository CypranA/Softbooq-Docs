CRM holds the people and businesses you work with, the deals you are working on, and what you did with them. Customers and suppliers are the same records Sales, Finance and Procurement use. CRM is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## How CRM is organised

Tabs: **Overview**, **Customers & Vendors**, **Contacts**, **Pipeline**, **Interactions**, **Tasks**, **Sequences**, **Lead Forms**, **Performance Analytics**, **Duplicates**.

Opening a customer shows their **Overview**, **Deals**, **Financials** and **Timeline**, the people who work there, and their **Customer account** (who can sign in to see their invoices and orders).

---

## Common tasks

### Add a customer

Open **Customers & Vendors** and choose **Add Customer**. **Check & Create** looks for an existing record with the same details before it creates a new one, so you do not end up with two.

### Work a deal

On **Pipeline**, choose **New Deal**, fill it in and **Launch Deal**. Move it through **Lead**, **Qualified**, **Proposal**, **Negotiation**, then **Won** or **Lost**; the stages are fixed. Closing a deal asks why it was won or lost, and reopening a closed deal asks for a reason too.

A deal does not turn into an invoice. Quotes and orders are made in [Sales & POS](/erp/sales), and invoices come from there.

### Record what happened

Use **Log Activity** for a call, meeting, email or note. Follow-ups go on **Tasks**.

### Merge duplicates

**Duplicates** lists records that look like the same customer. Merge them, swap which one is kept, or choose **Not a duplicate - dismiss**. For contacts, use **Review & merge** on the Contacts tab.

### Import and export

The **...** menu on Customers & Vendors has **Import** and **Export**.

---

## Relationship health

Each customer gets a health score from how recently you were in touch, overdue and paid invoices, deal momentum and active projects. It is kept up to date for you within about a minute of a change.
