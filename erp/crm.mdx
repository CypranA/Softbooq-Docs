The CRM module manages your entire customer relationship lifecycle — from first contact through to closed deal, active contract, and long-term account health.

## What CRM owns

- **Customers** — the master customer record shared across the entire ERP
- **Deals & pipelines** — track opportunities through your sales stages
- **Interactions** — log calls, meetings, emails, and notes against customers
- **Health scores** — automated scoring based on activity, spend, and engagement

## Data flows out of CRM to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Customer invoices |
| Sales & POS | Customer data for orders |
| Contracts | Customer contracts |
| Dashboard | Customer metrics |

## Common tasks

### Add a new customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM → Customers → New Customer" />
  <Step title="Fill in the customer details">
    Enter company name, primary contact, email, phone, and billing address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payment terms">
    Choose default payment terms (e.g. Net 30). These will pre-fill on every invoice raised for this customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The customer is now available across Finance, Sales, and Contracts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Create a deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM → Deals → New Deal" />
  <Step title="Link to a customer">
    Select the customer this deal is for. If they don't exist yet, create them first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set deal details">
    Enter the deal name, expected value, expected close date, and pipeline stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to a team member">
    Set the deal owner — the person responsible for closing it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move through stages">
    Drag the deal card across your pipeline as it progresses. Update the stage whenever the conversation moves forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Log an interaction

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a customer or deal record" />
  <Step title="Click Log Interaction">
    Choose the interaction type: call, meeting, email, or note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details">
    Write a summary of the conversation and any follow-up actions required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The interaction is timestamped and added to the customer's history. Your whole team can see it immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Customer profiles** — A single customer record that all modules reference. Changes in CRM reflect immediately across Sales, Finance, and Contracts.

**Deal pipeline** — Kanban-style pipeline view. Move deals through custom stages, set expected close dates, and track deal value.

**Interaction log** — Every call, email, meeting, and note is timestamped and linked to the customer record.

**Health scores** — Automatically calculated based on recency of interaction, payment history, contract value, and support ticket volume.
