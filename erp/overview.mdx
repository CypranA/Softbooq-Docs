Softbooq runs your whole business on one set of records. A sale in Sales & POS, a bill in Procurement or a payroll run in Human Resources all post to the same books, so every report reads from one place.

## The modules

Softbooq has 18 business modules. Which you have depends on your plan; who can open each one depends on their role.

| Module | What it does | Plan |
|--------|--------------|------|
| [Dashboard](/erp/overview) | Your day at a glance | Go |
| [Products & Services](/erp/inventory) | What you sell, stock in each store | Go |
| [Sales & POS](/erp/sales) | The register, orders, quotes, returns | Go |
| [Finance](/erp/finance) | Invoices, bills, expenses, your books | Go |
| [Procurement](/erp/procurement) | Suppliers, purchase orders, receiving | Go |
| [Resource Scheduling](/erp/scheduling) | Bookings for people, rooms and equipment | Go |
| [CRM](/erp/crm) | Customers, contacts, the deal pipeline | Plus |
| [Reports](/erp/reports) | Statements, VAT return, aging and more | Plus |
| [Projects](/erp/projects) | Tasks, time, budget and billing | Plus |
| [Travel & Expense](/erp/travel) | Trips, expense reports, reimbursements | Plus |
| [Asset Management](/erp/assets) | The asset register and depreciation | Plus |
| [Contract Management](/erp/contracts) | Contracts, e-signatures, milestones | Plus |
| [Leases & Obligations](/erp/leases) | Leases and recurring obligations | Plus |
| [Human Resources](/erp/hr) | Employees, time, leave, payroll, hiring | Pro |
| [IT & Helpdesk](/erp/it) | Tickets, knowledge base, devices | Pro |
| [Maintenance](/erp/maintenance) | Requests, work orders, the schedule | Pro |
| [Manufacturing](/erp/manufacturing) | BOMs, production runs, quality | Pro |
| [Compliance](/erp/compliance) | Policies and acknowledgments | Pro |

Each plan includes the ones before it: Plus includes everything in Go, Pro everything in Plus. Audit Logs are part of Softbooq Pro too. See [Subscription & Plans](/billing/subscription).

**User Management** (people, roles and the security policy), **Settings** and the **System Map**, a live diagram of how the modules connect, sit at the bottom of the sidebar.

On a phone the modules are grouped into **Core**, **Operations** and **Admin**. See [Softbooq on your phone](/erp/phone).

## Jacinta

Jacinta, Softbooq AI's assistant, is in every module: ask her about your business in plain words, and on problem screens she explains what is wrong. See [Jacinta](/erp/ai).

## The apps around Softbooq

One sign-in opens all of them, and they work on the same records:

- **Softbooq POS**: the register for the counter, which keeps working offline
- **Softbooq AI**: Jacinta in a full-page app, with projects, knowledge and scheduled reports
- **Softbooq BI**: dashboards and charts on your data
- **Softbooq Files**: your documents and connected cloud storage
- **Softbooq Studio**: free browser tools for invoices, quotes and more, with no account needed
