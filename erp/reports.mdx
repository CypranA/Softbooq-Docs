The Reports module provides pre-built and customisable reports drawing data from Finance, HR, Inventory, and Projects — giving you a single place to analyse business performance without exporting to spreadsheets.

## What Reports owns

- **Financial reports** — P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, aged receivables/payables
- **HR reports** — headcount, payroll summary, leave balances, attendance
- **Inventory reports** — stock valuation, movement history, low stock alerts

## Data flows into Reports from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Financial data |
| HR | Workforce data |
| Inventory | Stock data |
| Projects | Project data |

## Common tasks

### Run a Profit & Loss report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports → Financial → Profit & Loss" />
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Choose a start and end date, or select a preset period (This Month, This Quarter, This Year).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters (optional)">
    Filter by department or cost centre to see a segmented P&L.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View or export">
    Review the report on screen, or click **Export** to download as PDF or CSV.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Run an aged receivables report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports → Financial → Aged Receivables" />
  <Step title="Set the as-at date">
    Choose the date you want to see outstanding balances as of.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    The report groups outstanding invoices by age bucket: 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or act">
    Export for your accounting records, or click through to any invoice to send a payment reminder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Run a payroll summary report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports → HR → Payroll Summary" />
  <Step title="Select the period" />
  <Step title="Review">
    See total gross pay, deductions, and net pay by department and per employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Download as CSV for your payroll records or finance team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Check stock valuation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports → Inventory → Stock Valuation" />
  <Step title="Set the as-at date (optional)">
    Defaults to today. Change to see a historical snapshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    See the current value of your entire inventory, broken down by item and location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

**Financial reports** — P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Aged Receivables, Aged Payables — generated in real time from live Finance data.

**HR reports** — Headcount by department, monthly payroll summary, leave balances, and attendance analysis.

**Inventory reports** — Stock valuation, movement history, slow-moving items, and low-stock alerts.

**Custom date ranges** — All reports support custom date ranges, comparative periods, and department/project filters.

**Export** — Every report exports to PDF or CSV with one click.
