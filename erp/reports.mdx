Reports collects your figures in one place, straight from your books. Choose a report in the list on the left; the open report stays in the address, so you can share the link.

## The reports

| Group | Reports |
|-------|---------|
| **Overview** | Dashboard |
| **Financial Statements** | Financial Statements, VAT Return, UStVA by box number (Kennzahlen), OSS return, EC Sales List, P&L by Cost Center, Financials by Dimension, Lease Commitments (IFRS 16), Cash Reconciliation, Recon Workbench |
| **Receivables & Payables** | AR Aging, AP Aging, Early-Payment Savings, Payment Behaviour, GR/IR Aging, Sales Overview, Top Partners, Profitability, Channel Performance |
| **Operations** | Inventory, Procurement Spend, Fixed Asset Register, Production Overview |
| **HR & Payroll** | Workforce Report, Payroll Reports, Billable Hours, Gender Pay Gap |
| **Advanced Analytics** | Advanced Analytics |
| **Integrations** | By Integration Source |

The VAT return is named after your tax system, and it has its own page: [VAT return and Before you file](/erp/vat-return). The UStVA (for workspaces in Germany) and the OSS return (for workspaces in the EU) sort the same figures the way the tax office asks for them: [UStVA and the OSS return](/erp/ustva-oss).

## Financial statements

**Financial Statements** has tabs for the profit and loss statement, the balance sheet, cash flow, tax, equity, the trial balance and the notes. The names follow your accounting standard (for example "Statement of Financial Position" under IFRS). To change the standard, use **Change accounting standard** beside it; your chart of accounts stays as it is.

- Pick the period with **MTD**, **QTD**, **YTD**, **FY** or **Custom Range**. QTD, YTD and FY follow your financial year, which starts in the month set in [Settings](/erp/settings).
- Tick **Compare YoY** to see the same period last year beside it.
- Open any line to see the ledger entries behind it.

If a statement cannot be tied back to your ledger, Softbooq does not show figures it cannot stand behind: you see **Report withheld: cannot be reconciled to the ledger**. [Books Health](/erp/books-health) shows what is wrong.

## Who owes you, and since when

**AR Aging** sorts what customers owe into **Not yet due**, **0-30 Days**, **31-60 Days**, **61-90 Days** and **Over 90 Days**, plus **No due date** when an invoice has none. **AP Aging** does the same for what you owe suppliers.

## Matching the bank

**Recon Workbench** matches bank lines to invoices, bills and payments. Use **Run Auto-Match**, then confirm or reject each suggested match. **Cash Reconciliation** shows, per bank and cash account, whether it has been reconciled.

## Exporting

The **Export** menu at the top offers CSV or PDF, for the reports that provide them.

## Reports on a schedule

To have a report made for you on a schedule, set up a **Workflow** in the Softbooq AI app. Each run saves the report, with a short commentary from Jacinta on what changed, and notifies you. The figures come from your ledger; a set number of commentaries each month is included in your plan. See [Softbooq AI app](/platform/ai).
