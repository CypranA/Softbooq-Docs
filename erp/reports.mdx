title: Reports
description: Analytics and reporting across all modules
---

The Reports module provides pre-built and customisable reports drawing data from Finance, HR, Inventory, and Projects — giving you a single place to analyse business performance without exporting to spreadsheets.

## What Reports owns

- **Financial reports** — P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, aged receivables/payables
- **HR reports** — headcount, payroll summary, leave balances, attendance
- **Inventory reports** — stock valuation, movement history, low stock alerts

## Data flows into Reports from

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Finance | Financial data |
| HR | Workforce data |
| Inventory | Stock data |
| Projects | Project data |

## Key features

### Financial reports
Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Aged Receivables, and Aged Payables — all generated in real time from live Finance data. Export to PDF or CSV.

### HR reports
Headcount by department, monthly payroll summary, leave balance by employee, and attendance analysis.

### Inventory reports
Current stock valuation, stock movement history, slow-moving items, and low stock alerts by warehouse.

### Custom date ranges
All reports support custom date ranges, comparative periods, and department/project filters.
