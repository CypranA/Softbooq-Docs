The IT & Helpdesk module tracks your company's technology assets and manages internal support requests — giving your IT team visibility over the full hardware estate and a structured queue for resolving issues.

## What IT & Helpdesk owns

- **IT assets** — computers, phones, peripherals, and other technology hardware
- **Support tickets** — internal helpdesk requests from employees

## Data flows out of IT & Helpdesk to

| Module | What it sends |
|--------|--------------|
| Assets | IT asset records for the fixed asset register |

## Key features

### IT asset tracking
Maintain a register of every IT asset — serial number, assigned employee, purchase date, warranty expiry, and current status. Assets are linked to the main Assets module for depreciation and disposal.

### Support tickets
Employees submit IT support requests through the helpdesk. Tickets are assigned to IT team members, prioritised, and tracked through to resolution.

### Ticket queues
Organise tickets by category (hardware, software, network, access), priority, and assignee. SLA timers track response and resolution time.

### Asset assignment
Link IT assets directly to employees. When an employee leaves (via HR), the system flags their assigned assets for retrieval.
