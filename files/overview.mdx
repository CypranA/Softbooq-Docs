Softbooq Files brings your company's documents together: the files kept with your Softbooq records, and the cloud drives you already use, such as SharePoint, Google Drive and Dropbox. One search finds a file wherever it is, and Jacinta can answer questions about the documents you give her.

Open it from the app switcher, with the same sign-in as the rest of Softbooq.

## Your company files

Under **Files** in the sidebar are your company's own folders, stored by Softbooq.

- **Files kept with your records**, such as invoices, contracts and employee documents, are managed from the module they belong to. In Files you can open and read them; the folder shows **This folder is managed by the ERP. Open the ERP to manage these files.** with a button to **Open ERP**.
- **AI Knowledge** is the folder for documents Jacinta should know about. Employees, Managers and Admins can upload, rename and delete files there.

### Who can see a folder

Managers and Admins can right-click a company folder and choose **Manage access**. A folder is open to **Everyone with file access**, or **Restricted** to chosen **Departments**, **Roles** or **Specific people**. Managers and Admins always have access.

Right-click also offers **Version history** and **Properties**.

## Connect your cloud drives

Add **SharePoint**, **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **Dropbox**, **Box** or a **Local Folder**. With **Add for whole organisation**, a drive is added for everyone, either with each member signing in with their own account, or with one shared company account.

Where a drive reports its size, Files shows how much of it is used.

## Search

**Search everything (Ctrl+K)** searches your files across all your drives at once.

## Jacinta and your documents

Choose **Add to AI Knowledge** on a file, or **Add folder to AI Knowledge** on a folder, and Jacinta can use it when she answers. Knowledge bases are organised in the ERP under **Settings → Softbooq AI**, where each one is a folder inside **AI Knowledge**.

## Keeping and deleting files

In Files' settings, under **Retention**:

- **Statutory retention**: financial documents such as invoices, vendor bills, receipts and payslips cannot be permanently deleted before the legal period ends.
- **Recycle Bin**: deleted files are kept in **Trash** for 30 days, then removed for good.
- **Legal holds**: the files under a hold. Only Admins can **Lift hold**.

An Admin can put a single file on hold under **Properties** with **Legal hold**: it can then never be permanently deleted or removed automatically. To put the whole workspace on hold, use the **Legal hold** card in the ERP's **Settings → Legal & Compliance**.

## Settings

Files' settings have four tabs: **Connected Drives**, **Preferences**, **Retention** and **Enterprise Auth**.
