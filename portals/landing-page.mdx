Every Softbooq workspace has a public site at `<your-handle>.mysoftbooq.com`. Its home page introduces your business and links to your online store, careers page and contact details. The same site carries your [online store](/portals/storefront), [careers page](/portals/careers), [customer account](/portals/customer-account) and help centre.

Everything about it is set in **Settings → Business → Public Page**.

## What you can set

- **Hero Section**: the headline and **Subtitle / Description** at the top
- **Branding & Call to Action**: your brand colour and the main button, with its text and link (a URL, or a section of the page such as `#contact`)
- **About Section** and **Trust & Compliance Section**: a few words about you, and what you want visitors to trust you for
- **Section Visibility**: show or hide **About**, **Services**, **Contact**, **Store** and **Careers**
- **Business Status**: **Online Status**, and a **Custom Status Message** such as "Closed for Holidays"
- **Social & Contact Links**: your website and social profiles, and a contact email and phone that override your business details

Your brand colour is one colour for everything: the store, the careers page, the browser tab on phones, and your invoices and emails.

## Your address

Your handle is set under **Public address**. If you change it, your public pages answer on the new address straight away, and the old one keeps working: it stays reserved to you, so links already shared, bookmarked or printed still work.

## Search engines

**Search Engine Visibility** decides whether your landing page, shop and careers page can appear in Google, Bing and other search engines. Customer accounts and document-verification links always stay private and are never indexed.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own domain?">
    Not at the moment. Your public site is served at `<your-handle>.mysoftbooq.com`. You can link to it from your own website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the Careers section not showing?">
    It needs Careers switched on under Section Visibility, and Human Resources on your plan (Softbooq Pro).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own code or tracking scripts?">
    No. Your public site does not accept scripts or custom HTML, which keeps every business's site and its visitors safe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
