Your customers have their own account on your public site, at `<your-handle>.mysoftbooq.com/account`. They sign in with a 6-digit code sent to their email. There is no password and no user licence to buy: customers are not users of your workspace.

## What your customers see

- **Invoices**: pay online, download the PDF or the e-invoice
- **Orders**: track them, and **Order again**
- **Quotes**: respond to them
- Contracts, credit notes, returns, payment plans, reminders and bookings
- **Support requests**: raise one, reply, and rate the answer (Softbooq Pro)
- Projects (Softbooq Plus and Pro) and documents you share with them
- Saved card or direct debit, and **Your details**
- **Your data**: download a copy, or ask for their data to be erased

Each section shows only what belongs to that customer. The customer account itself is included on every plan.

---

## Give someone access

Access is managed on the customer's record in CRM, in the **Customer account** panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer in CRM">
    The **Customer account** panel lists who can sign in, and when they last did.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email under Email address to invite, then Invite">
    They receive an email with a sign-in link. **Copy sign-in link** gives you the same link to send yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Anyone using the email address on the customer record can also sign in on their own, unless self sign-in is switched off for your workspace. Then only people you invite can sign in.

To take access away, choose **Remove access**. They are signed out on their next click.
