Jacinta, Softbooq's AI assistant, has two modes. **Fast** is free. **Advanced** uses credits from your workspace's AI wallet. One wallet is shared across Softbooq, the Softbooq AI app, Softbooq BI and Softbooq Files.

## Fast and Advanced

You choose the mode in the chat panel, next to your credit balance.

| Mode | What it does | Cost |
|------|--------------|------|
| Fast | Finds your records and answers your totals | Free, on every plan |
| Advanced | Filters, compares and prepares work for you | Usually 1 credit per message |

An Advanced message is charged by the work it actually needed. Most cost 1 credit. A heavy question that pulls a lot of data can cost a few more, and one message can **never cost more than 10 credits** (3 during a trial). **Deep analysis** in the Softbooq AI app digs further for questions like "why did margin fall?" and is capped at 30 credits; it is not available during a trial.

If a message would go over its cap, it stops, the credits it held are refunded, and you get the part of the answer it had.

## Credits that come with your plan

- During the 30-day trial your workspace gets **2 credits** to try Advanced. During a trial Jacinta answers but does not take actions.
- When you subscribe, you get a one-time bonus: **5 credits** on Softbooq Go, **20** on Softbooq Plus, **50** on Softbooq Pro.

---

## Keeping spending in check

In **Settings → Softbooq AI**, Admins set the **AI credit policy**:

- **Daily limit per user**: recommended, so one long session cannot drain the shared wallet.
- **Monthly budget per user**: optional; 0 means no monthly cap.
- **Set individual caps and see usage** for a single person.

When someone reaches their limit or the wallet is empty, they can **Request more credits**. Admins see these under **Pending AI credit requests** and choose **Approve** or **Deny**: approving a limit request lifts that person's cap; a workspace top-up needs a payment (**Top up**).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Fast really free?">
    Yes, on every plan. Fast finds records and answers totals without using credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did one Advanced message use more than 1 credit?">
    It needed more work than a usual question, for example comparing several modules at once. It still cannot go over 10 credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when the wallet is empty?">
    Fast keeps working. Advanced asks you to top up, or to request more credits from your admins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are AI credits part of my plan price?">
    No. Your plan is priced per user. Credits are a separate wallet, and the free Fast mode needs none.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
