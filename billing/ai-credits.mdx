Jacinta, Softbooq AI's assistant, has two engines. **Fast** is free. **Advanced**, which can compare across your records, prepare work for you and take actions, uses AI credits. Every paying workspace gets credits with its plan each month, can buy more in packs, and shares them between Softbooq AI and Softbooq BI.

## What is free

- **Fast** answers: finding your records and telling you your totals. A workspace can ask up to 200 Fast questions a day; the count resets at midnight UTC.
- **Jacinta on problem screens**, such as Books Health or a payment run that will not go through: 20 included conversations a day per workspace. When the problem is a fault in Softbooq rather than in your books, it is always free.
- **Workflow commentaries**: 5 a month per workspace.

## What Advanced costs

Each Advanced message costs **1 credit**. **Deep analysis**, which investigates across many steps, costs **5 credits**. That is all a message ever costs, however much work the answer takes. A request that would run far too long stops on its own, its credits are given back, and Jacinta asks you to make the question more specific.

During a free trial, Deep analysis is not available.

## Credits every month

Every paying workspace gets credits with its plan at the start of each month:

| Plan | Credits a month |
|------|----------------:|
| Softbooq Go | 50 |
| Softbooq Plus | 150 |
| Softbooq Pro | 400 |

- They are used **before** the credits you bought.
- They last until the end of the month and renew on the 1st (UTC). Unused monthly credits do not carry over; credits you bought always stay.
- A workspace that starts paying in the middle of a month gets the whole month's credits at once.
- Moving to a bigger plan adds the difference straight away. Moving to a smaller one keeps what this month already gave.
- A free trial has no monthly credits: it starts with 2.

## Packs

| Pack | Credits | Price |
|------|--------:|------:|
| 100 credits | 100 | €10 / $10 |
| 500 credits | 500 | €40 / $40 |
| 1,000 credits | 1,000 | €70 / $70 |

Credits you buy never expire. Each workspace also gets a one-time bonus: 5 credits on Softbooq Go, 20 on Plus, 50 on Pro. A workspace on its free trial gets 2 of them at once and the rest when its first payment clears.

## Buy credits and see your balance

Only Admins can buy credits.

- In Softbooq AI, **Wallet** shows the **Credits to spend**, how many of this month's **Monthly credits** are left and when they renew, your **Bought credits**, and **Recent transactions**. **Top up** opens the packs.
- In the ERP, **Settings → Softbooq AI** shows the balance and this month's credits, with **Top up credits**.
- In the Admin Portal, **Billing** shows the **AI Credits** available, with **Top Up**.
- Where it is offered for your country, a pack can also be paid with **Pay by bank transfer or mobile money**. The invoice, the details to pay into and **Send proof of payment** are then in the Admin Portal under **Billing**.

## Keeping spending in check

Each person can use up to 10 credits a day, to start with. Admins can change that per person in Softbooq AI's **Usage** page (a **Daily cap** and a **Monthly cap**), and set the default under the ERP's **Settings → Softbooq AI**.

When someone reaches their limit, Jacinta tells them, and their allowance comes back at midnight UTC (or next month, for the monthly cap).

## When credits run out

When a workspace has fewer than 10 credits left, its Admins get an alert and an email, **AI credits running low**, once each time it runs low. Each Admin can switch it off under **Your alerts**.

When there are none left, Advanced stops until someone tops up or the month's credits renew on the 1st. In the ERP, people can choose **Request more credits** to let their Admins know. Fast keeps working.
