Softbooq is priced per user who signs in. You pick one plan for your workspace and pay for the people who use it. AI credits are a separate wallet (see [AI Credits](/billing/ai-credits)).

## Plans

| Plan | Price per user | What it adds |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Softbooq Go | $7.99 / month | Dashboard, Products & Services, Sales & POS, Finance, Procurement, Resource Scheduling |
| Softbooq Plus | $10.99 / month | Everything in Go, plus CRM, Reports, Projects, Travel & Expense, Asset Management, Contract Management, Leases & Obligations |
| Softbooq Pro | $13.99 / month | Everything in Plus, plus Human Resources, IT & Helpdesk, Maintenance, Manufacturing, Compliance, Audit Logs |

- Paying yearly costs ten months: $79.90, $109.90 and $139.90 per user per year.
- Prices in euro are the same numbers (for example €7.99 per user per month on Go).
- One paid user is one person who signs in. Employee records, POS registers and cashiers are unlimited and never count towards it.
- There are no user caps and no storage tiers on any plan.
- For larger organisations there is an Enterprise option with custom pricing. Contact us from [softbooq.com/pricing](https://softbooq.com/pricing).

---

## The 30-day free trial

A new workspace starts a **30-day free trial** on the plan you picked at sign-up, or on Softbooq Pro if you did not pick one. Nothing is charged during the trial, and you can switch plans as often as you like, including up to Pro to see everything.

If the trial ends without a paid plan, the workspace is cancelled 14 days later. Your records are then kept for 90 days, and choosing a plan in that time brings everything back as it was.

## Change your plan or seats

Plans and seats are managed in the **Admin Portal** at accounts.softbooq.com.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Billing & Plans → Open Admin Portal">
    The Admin Portal has the tabs Billing, Seats, Apps, Integrations and Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billing: choose Upgrade or Downgrade on a plan">
    Before anything changes, **Confirm Plan Change** shows exactly what you will be charged today for the rest of the current period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seats: Add Seats">
    Enter the new seat count. The dialog shows what is due today before you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Card, invoices and cancelling

**Manage Billing** (in Settings → Billing & Plans, and in the Admin Portal) opens the Stripe billing portal. There you update your card, download invoices, or cancel your plan. Softbooq never sees your full card number.

After a cancellation your records are kept for 90 days. Renew any time before that date and everything comes back exactly as it is. After it, the records are permanently deleted.

To take a copy of your books at any time, use **Settings → Billing & Plans → Export your accounting records → Download records**: the general ledger plus your statutory audit file (SAF-T or DATEV).

---

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, your workspace enters a **14-day grace period**. Update your card through **Manage Billing** and the next attempt uses it.
