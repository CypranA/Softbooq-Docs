Connect your Shopify store and Softbooq imports its products, orders and customers, then keeps them up to date as they change in Shopify. Shopify orders arrive in Sales & POS as online orders, and stock you change in Softbooq is sent back to Shopify.

## Connect your store

You need to be an Admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Under **Commerce**, find the **Shopify** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Connect Shopify">
    Enter your store's address. The short name is enough: `my-store` works as well as `my-store.myshopify.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Continue to Shopify">
    Shopify asks you to approve the connection. Once you do, you come back to Softbooq and the tile shows your store as connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first import

After connecting, choose **Import options** (or **Choose** on the **First import?** banner) to open **Import from Shopify**:

- **What to import**: **Products**, **Orders** and **Customers**, all ticked to start with
- **How far back**: **All history**, **Last 12 months**, **Last 90 days** (the default), **Last 30 days** or **Only new from now**

Then choose **Start import**. Each run brings in up to 12,500 products, 12,500 orders and 12,500 customers. If your store has more, the tile says **Partial sync**, and running **Sync products** (or orders, or customers) again fetches the next batch.

## What stays in step

| What | How it moves |
|------|--------------|
| Products | From Shopify, as they are created, changed or deleted |
| Orders | From Shopify, as they are placed and updated. They appear in Sales & POS with the store shown as **Online** |
| Draft orders | From Shopify, as quotes. A deleted draft order cancels its quote |
| Customers | From Shopify, as they are created, changed or deleted. Softbooq does not send customers to Shopify |
| Stock changed in Shopify | Recorded in Softbooq as a stock movement |
| Stock changed in Softbooq | Sent to Shopify within 2 minutes, while **Auto-push on** is set |

Shopify tells Softbooq about each change as it happens. As a safety net, Softbooq also checks products, orders and customers every 6 hours, so nothing is missed if a message from Shopify is lost.

A product deleted in Shopify is removed from your product list in Softbooq.

### Stock sent to Shopify

Under **Outbound stock push**, **Auto-push on** sends the quantity you hold in Softbooq to Shopify, in whole units, at one Shopify location. Turn it to **Auto-push off** if Shopify should stay the place you manage stock. **Location mappings** decide which Softbooq store receives stock changes that come from each Shopify location.

## Fulfilling and returning Shopify orders

- **Fulfil**: open a paid Shopify order in Sales & POS and use **Fulfill in Shopify**. Add the **Tracking #**, **Carrier** and **Tracking URL** if you have them, then choose **Mark as Fulfilled**. Shopify notifies the customer with the tracking details.
- **Return**: processing a return on a Shopify order records the returned items in Shopify and puts them back in stock there. Refund the money itself in Shopify. Items count as restocked in Softbooq only when Shopify says they were restocked.

## Sync conflicts

If a product or customer was edited in Softbooq after the last sync and Shopify then sends a different value, Softbooq does not overwrite your edit. The **Sync conflicts** panel lists each one, with the field, its value **In ERP** and its value **From Shopify**. Choose **Use Shopify** or **Keep ERP** for each.
