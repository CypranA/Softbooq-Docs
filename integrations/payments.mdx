A payment run pays many bills, expense claims and wages at once. You choose what to pay, someone approves it, and then the money goes out through Wise, through Revolut, or through a payment file you upload to your bank. When the run is confirmed, everything in it is marked paid and posted to your books.

## Choose how you pay

In **Settings → Banking → Send Payments / Payouts**:

- **Bank File Export** needs no setup and is always on. Softbooq makes a file in your bank's format and you upload it in your online banking.
- **Wise Business**: enter an **API Token** (in Wise: Settings → Developer tools → API tokens) and your business **Profile ID**, then **Verify Connection** and **Save Wise Settings**.
- **Revolut Business**: enter an **API Token** (in Revolut Business: Settings → API → Personal API key) and the **Account ID** of the account to pay from, then **Verify Connection** and **Save Revolut Settings**.

With both Wise and Revolut connected, **Default outbound rail** picks which one is shown first in payment runs.

**Remittance advice to suppliers** is on to start with: when a run is paid, each supplier is emailed what the payment covers, the total and the bank reference.

## Make a payment run

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Finance → Purchases → Payment Runs">
    Choose **New Payment Run**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to pay">
    Give the run a name if you like, the **Scheduled Payment Date** and the account to **Pay from**. Then choose from:

    - **Bills** that are due and not yet paid (a bill can be paid in part)
    - **POs** that have been received or billed
    - **Expenses** that have been approved
    - **Reimbursements** waiting to be paid
    - **Payroll**: wages not yet paid
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    The run starts as a draft. **Approve** it. Someone other than the person who made the run approves it, unless only one person in your workspace can approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    - Through Wise or Revolut: choose **Pay via Wise** or **Pay via Revolut**. Use **Sync Wise** or **Sync Revolut** to check on the transfers. Once they have all gone through, the run confirms itself.
    - Through your bank: choose **Export** and a format (SEPA XML, BACS CSV, NACHA ACH, ABA (Cemtex) or Generic CSV), upload the file in your online banking, and once your bank has paid it, choose **Confirm Paid**.
    - Already paid another way: **Already paid these** confirms the run without a file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Confirming asks you to verify it is you again. For payments in another currency, it also asks you to confirm the exchange rates.

### Your bank's own layout

If your bank wants a file none of the standard formats match, use **Bank file layout** (or **Set up my bank's layout...** in the **Export** list) to describe it once. It then appears at the top of the **Export** list.

## Cancel a run

Until it is confirmed, a run can be cancelled with **Cancel Run**, with a reason if you like.

## Who can do this

Seeing payment runs is one permission. Making, approving, paying and cancelling them is another, so someone can follow runs without being able to send money.
