Connect your WooCommerce store and Softbooq imports its products, orders and customers, then keeps them up to date as they change. WooCommerce orders arrive in Sales & POS as online orders, and stock you change in Softbooq is sent back to your store.

## Connect your store

You need to be an Admin, and a REST API key from WooCommerce.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key in WooCommerce">
    In your WordPress admin, open **WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API** and add a key with **Read/Write** access. Keep the page open: the secret is shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Under **Commerce**, find the **WooCommerce** tile and choose **Connect WooCommerce**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store's details">
    Fill in **Store URL** (for example `https://my-store.com`), **Consumer Key** and **Consumer Secret**, then choose **Connect**. Softbooq checks the key with your store straight away and tells you if it does not work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first import

Open **Import from WooCommerce**:

- **What to import**: products, orders and customers
- **How far back**: **All history**, **Last 12 months**, **Last 90 days** (the default), **Last 30 days** or **Only new from now**

Each run brings in up to 5,000 products, 5,000 orders and 5,000 customers. For a larger store, run **Sync products**, **Sync orders** or **Sync customers** again to fetch the next batch.

## What stays in step

| What | How it moves |
|------|--------------|
| Products | From WooCommerce, as they are created, changed or deleted |
| Orders | From WooCommerce, in every status, as they are placed and updated. They appear in Sales & POS with the store shown as **Online** |
| Customers | From WooCommerce, as they are created, changed or deleted |
| Stock changed in Softbooq | Sent to WooCommerce within 2 minutes, while **Auto-push on** is set |

Your store tells Softbooq about each change as it happens, and Softbooq also checks every 6 hours so nothing is missed. **Resync all stock to WooCommerce** sends every product's stock again, which is useful after a large stock count.

### How order statuses arrive

| In WooCommerce | In Softbooq |
|----------------|-------------|
| Pending payment, On hold | Pending |
| Processing, not yet paid | Pending |
| Processing, paid | Paid |
| Completed | Completed |
| Cancelled, Failed | Cancelled |
| Refunded | Refunded |

## Fulfilling and returning WooCommerce orders

- **Fulfil**: open a paid WooCommerce order in Sales & POS and use **Fulfill in WooCommerce**, then **Mark as Fulfilled**. WooCommerce marks the order as completed.
- **Return**: processing a return on a WooCommerce order records the refund in your store and puts the items back in stock there. Pay the money back through your payment provider or in WooCommerce.

## Sync conflicts

If a product or customer was edited in Softbooq after the last sync and your store then sends a different value, Softbooq does not overwrite your edit. The **Sync conflicts** panel lists each one: choose **Use WooCommerce** or **Keep ERP**. On a phone, conflicts are resolved on a computer.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plan do I need?">
    WooCommerce works on every plan. Customers are imported into CRM, which is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The connection was refused. What should I check?">
    That the **Store URL** is your store's public address, and that the key was created with **Read/Write** access. A key with Read access only cannot receive stock or fulfilments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
