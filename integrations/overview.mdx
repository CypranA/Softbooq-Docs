Softbooq connects to the stores, accounting tools, CRMs, project tools and devices your business already uses. Most connections are in **Settings → Integrations**. Email, messaging and banking have their own places in Settings.

You need permission to manage Settings to see these screens, and to be an Admin to connect most tools.

## In Settings → Integrations

| Group | Tools |
|-------|-------|
| **Identity & Directory** | Google Workspace Directory, Microsoft Entra ID Directory. See [Directory sync](/integrations/admin-sync) |
| **Commerce** | [Shopify](/integrations/shopify), [WooCommerce](/integrations/woocommerce) |
| **Accounting** | [Xero and QuickBooks Online](/integrations/xero), and [lexoffice, Pennylane, Zoho Books, DATEV, sevdesk and MoneyBird](/integrations/byok-accounting) |
| **CRM** | [HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot), Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM |
| **Project Management** | Microsoft Planner, Asana, Trello, Jira, Linear, Zoho Projects. See [Project tools](/integrations/project-tools). And [Confluence](/integrations/confluence) |
| **Device Management** | Microsoft Intune, Jamf Pro, Google Workspace Devices. See [Device management](/integrations/device-management) |
| **Travel** | TravelPerk (needs a TravelPerk Pro subscription), [Airalo eSIMs](/integrations/esim) |
| **HR** | Personio |

The chips at the top of the screen filter by group.

### Paid add-ons

Xero, QuickBooks Online and DATEV are paid add-ons. Click the tile, tell us why you need it and **Submit request**. We review requests within one business day and email you when it is approved.

## Elsewhere in Settings

| Where | What |
|-------|------|
| **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service** | Who sends your emails: Softbooq, Brevo, SendGrid, Google, Microsoft 365 or your own mail server. See [Sending email](/integrations/email) |
| **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Instant Messaging** | Text messages through Twilio, and WhatsApp |
| **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Team Channels** | Notifications to Slack or Microsoft Teams |
| **Settings → Banking → Bank Feeds** | Your bank transactions, through Enable Banking. See [Bank Feeds](/integrations/bank-feeds) |
| **Settings → Banking → Receive Payments** | Card payments from your customers through Stripe |
| **Settings → Banking → Send Payments / Payouts** | Paying suppliers through Wise, Revolut or a bank file. See [Paying suppliers and staff](/integrations/payments) |

## In the modules and apps

- **Calendars**: in Resource Scheduling, **My Calendars** connects your Google or Outlook calendar.
- **Cloud drives**: Softbooq Files connects SharePoint, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box and local folders. See [Softbooq Files](/files/overview).

## When a connection has a problem

A tile shows **Error** when the last sync failed. Your Dashboard lists the records that could not be sent and why, with buttons to open the integration, its mappings, or reconnect.
