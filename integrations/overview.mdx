Softbooq connects to the stores, accounting tools, CRMs, project tools and devices your business already uses. Most connections are in **Settings → Integrations**. Email, messaging and banking have their own places in Settings.

You need permission to manage Settings to see these screens, and to be an Admin to connect most tools.

## In Settings → Integrations

<Frame caption="The chips at the top filter by group. A tool marked Coming soon cannot be connected yet.">
  <img src="/images/erp/integrations.jpg" alt="Settings, Integrations: the group chips and the integration tiles" />
</Frame>

| Group | Tools you can connect |
|-------|-------|
| **Commerce** | [Shopify](/integrations/shopify), [WooCommerce](/integrations/woocommerce) |
| **Accounting** | [Xero](/integrations/xero), and [lexoffice, sevdesk and MoneyBird](/integrations/byok-accounting) |
| **CRM** | [HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot) |
| **Project Management** | Asana and Trello. See [Project tools](/integrations/project-tools) |
| **Travel** | [Airalo eSIMs](/integrations/esim) |

The chips at the top of the screen filter by group.

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** These show **Coming soon** in Settings and cannot be connected yet. A workspace that connected one before keeps it.

  - **Identity & Directory**: Google Workspace Directory and Microsoft Entra ID Directory ([Directory sync](/integrations/admin-sync))
  - **Accounting**: QuickBooks Online, Pennylane, Zoho Books and DATEV
  - **CRM**: Salesforce, Pipedrive and Zoho CRM
  - **Project Management**: Microsoft Planner, Jira, Linear, Zoho Projects and [Confluence](/integrations/confluence)
  - **Device Management**: Microsoft Intune, Jamf Pro and Google Workspace Devices ([Device management](/integrations/device-management))
  - **Travel**: TravelPerk
  - **HR**: Personio
  - **Email and messaging**: SendGrid, Google and Microsoft 365 for sending email; Twilio and WhatsApp for messages
  - **Calendars**: Google Calendar and Outlook
</Note>

### Xero on request

Xero is free. Click the **Xero** tile, tell us why you need it and **Submit request**. We switch it on for you, usually within one working day. There is no charge.

## Elsewhere in Settings

| Where | What |
|-------|------|
| **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service** | Who sends your emails: Softbooq, your own domain, Brevo, or your own mail server. See [Sending email](/integrations/email) |
| **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Team Channels** | Notifications to Slack or Microsoft Teams |
| **Settings → Banking → Bank Feeds** | Your bank transactions, through Enable Banking. See [Bank Feeds](/integrations/bank-feeds) |
| **Settings → Banking → Receive Payments** | Card payments from your customers through Stripe. See [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) |
| **Settings → Banking → Send Payments / Payouts** | Paying suppliers through Wise, Revolut or a bank file. See [Paying suppliers and staff](/integrations/payments) |

## In the modules and apps

- **Cloud drives**: Softbooq Files connects SharePoint, Google Drive, OneDrive (a personal or a work account), Dropbox, Box and local folders. See [Softbooq Files](/files/overview).

## When a connection has a problem

A tile shows **Error** when the last sync failed. Your Dashboard lists the records that could not be sent and why, with buttons to open the integration, its mappings, or reconnect.
