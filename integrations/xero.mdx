Connect Xero and Softbooq sends your invoices there, brings back your customers, and marks invoices paid when the payment is recorded in Xero. Purchase orders and their payments go across too.

Xero is in **Settings → Integrations**, under **Accounting**. It is free: you request access and we switch it on for you. You need to be an Admin to connect it.

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** QuickBooks Online shows **Coming soon** in Settings and cannot be connected yet. The QuickBooks column below shows what it will do.
</Note>


## Get access and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Click the **Xero** tile. Tell us in **Reason (optional)** why you need it and choose **Submit request**. We switch it on for you, usually within one working day, and there is no charge. Until then, the tile shows **Request submitted**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Choose **Connect Xero** and sign in. If your Xero login has several organisations, Softbooq connects to the first one and tells you so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your tax codes and cost centres">
    Under **Integration Mappings**, the **Tax Codes** and **Cost Centers** tabs say which code in Xero each Softbooq tax rate and cost centre becomes. Choose **Save mappings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Xero starts its first sync as soon as you connect.

## What goes across

| What | Xero | QuickBooks Online (coming soon) |
|------|------|-------------------|
| Customers | Brought back from Xero | Brought back from QuickBooks |
| Invoices | Sent from Softbooq | Sent from Softbooq |
| Invoice payments | Brought back, marking the invoice paid | Brought back, marking the invoice paid |
| Purchase orders | Approved and sent orders are sent as bills | Not sent |
| Payments of those bills | Brought back against the purchase order | Not brought back |
| Chart of accounts | Brought back | Not brought back |

A draft invoice arrives in Xero as a draft. Once it has been sent, it arrives approved.

Everything runs every night. You can also run each part yourself from the panel: **Sync contacts**, **Push invoices**, **Pull payments** and **Push bills**. Invoices from the last 90 days are sent the first time.

## When something does not go across

- The panel shows the last error in red under that row, and the tile shows **Error**.
- Your Dashboard lists each record that could not be sent and why, with buttons to **Open integration**, **Open mappings** or **Reconnect**. Correct the record and send it again.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops the sync and ends Softbooq's access at Xero. If Xero cannot be reached, nothing is disconnected and you are told, so you can try again. Invoices already there stay there, and you can reconnect later.
