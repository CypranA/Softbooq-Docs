Bank feeds bring your bank account's transactions into Softbooq every few hours, so you can match each payment to the invoice, bill or expense it settles. Banks across the SEPA countries and the UK connect through Enable Banking. If you would rather not connect your bank, you can upload a statement file instead.

Bank feeds are part of Finance, on every plan.

## Before you start: your Enable Banking app

Softbooq connects to your bank through your own Enable Banking application. Enable Banking bills you for it directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register an application at Enable Banking">
    In Enable Banking's control panel, register an application. It gives you an application ID and a private key file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Banking → Bank Feeds">
    Under **Enable Banking - API credentials**, choose the **Environment** (**Production** for your real bank, **Sandbox** to try it out).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Paste the **App ID**, and the whole private key file into **RSA Private Key (PEM)**, including its BEGIN and END lines. Choose **Save credentials**, then **Test credentials** to check them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You need permission to manage Finance to change these.

## Connect a bank account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Connect Bank">
    In **Add a bank account**, pick the **Country** and your bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Authorise">
    You are sent to your bank to approve access. The access is read-only: Softbooq can see transactions but cannot make payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the GL Account">
    Pick the account in your chart of accounts that this bank account posts to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first sync brings in the last 30 days. After that, new transactions arrive every 6 hours. You can also sync at any time with the sync button next to the connection, or **Sync** in Finance.

Your bank's approval lasts 90 days. After that, connect the account again to keep transactions coming.

## Match transactions

Transactions land in **Finance → Accounting → Bank Feeds**, where you can filter them as **Unmatched**, **Auto-matched**, **Matched** or **Excluded**:

- **Match** links a transaction to the record it pays, then **Confirm match**
- **Unmatch** undoes a match
- **Exclude** takes a transaction out of reconciliation, such as a transfer between your own accounts. **Put back in reconciliation** brings it back

Every night, Softbooq matches what it can by itself. On Softbooq Plus and Pro, **Reports → Recon Workbench** shows those matches with how sure Softbooq was, so you can **Confirm match** or **Reject match**, link the rest with **Link manually**, and run **Run Auto-Match** whenever you like. **Needs Review** counts the matches Softbooq was least sure of.

## Upload a statement instead

In **Finance → Accounting → Reconciliation**, choose **Upload Statement** and pick a file from your bank: CSV, CAMT.053, MT940, OFX or QIF. You then match its transactions on the same screen.

## Disconnect

Disconnecting stops new transactions coming in. Transactions already imported stay. To withdraw the access at your bank too, do it in your online banking.
