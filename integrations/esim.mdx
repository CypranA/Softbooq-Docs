Before a trip abroad, you can buy an eSIM data plan in Softbooq for yourself or a colleague. The plans come from Airalo. The purchase is recorded as a travel expense, and the eSIM is installed on the phone with a QR code. eSIMs are part of Travel & Expense, in Softbooq Plus and Pro. There is nothing to connect: the plans are ready to buy.

## Buy an eSIM

Admins, Managers and Employees can buy eSIMs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Travel & Expense → Bookings">
    Choose **eSIM**, next to **Flights**, **Stays** and **Cars**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan in Browse Plans">
    Prices are in US dollars. The list is refreshed every hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Buy eSIM">
    - **Order for employee**: you, or the colleague who is travelling
    - **Attach to trip (optional)**: links the expense to a trip, with its cost centre and project
    - **Send activation to employee email**: ticked to start with. Airalo then emails the traveller the installation details as a PDF
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Confirm Order">
    The eSIM appears under **My eSIMs**. A colleague you ordered for also gets a notification in Softbooq.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install it

Open the eSIM under **My eSIMs** for **eSIM Activation**:

- Scan the QR code with the phone's camera, or
- On an iPhone, choose **Install on iPhone** when it is offered, or
- Install by hand with the **ICCID** and **LPA (Manual install)** codes, each with a copy button

Installing needs an internet connection, so install it before you leave.

## Data left and top-ups

**Refresh usage** shows how much data is left and when the plan expires. Before the eSIM is first used, it says **Activate eSIM to view usage**.

To add data to an eSIM that is **Active**, choose **Top Up**, pick a package and **Confirm Top-up**.

An eSIM is **Not active** until it is installed and used, then **Active**, and **Finished** or **Expired** when its data or time runs out.

## How it is paid and recorded

- **Paying**: the price is added to your next Softbooq invoice. During a free trial, you pay by card when you order, in **Complete Payment**. Top-ups always go on the invoice.
- **The expense**: each purchase and top-up becomes a travel expense for the traveller, with status Pending, so it goes through your usual expense approval.
- **The books**: it is posted to the travel expense account in your chart of accounts, against what you owe Softbooq.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is the price in US dollars?">
    Airalo prices its plans in US dollars. The expense also carries the amount in your workspace currency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see Bookings">
    Bookings needs Travel & Expense on your plan (Softbooq Plus or Pro) and a role that may book travel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
