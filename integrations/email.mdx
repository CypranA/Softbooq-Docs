Softbooq emails invoices, quotes, payment reminders and notifications on your behalf. Out of the box it sends them for you, up to a monthly limit. To send from your own address or send more, connect Brevo, SendGrid, Google, Microsoft 365 or your own mail server.

Everything is in **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service**.

## Who the email comes from

Under **Notification Email**, set the **Display Name** your customers see, and the **From Address** for providers that send from an address you choose.

## Choose a provider

Under **Choose Email Provider**:

| Provider | What you enter |
|----------|----------------|
| **Default** | Nothing. Softbooq sends for you, up to a number of free emails each month |
| **Brevo** (Recommended) | An **API Key** from Brevo |
| **SendGrid** | An **API Key** from SendGrid. Verify your sender in SendGrid first |
| **Google** | **SMTP Host**, **Port**, **Username** and **Password**. Use `smtp.gmail.com`, port 587, and an app password from your Google account rather than your normal password |
| **Microsoft 365** | The **Sender Mailbox** and your **Microsoft Tenant ID**, then **Connect Microsoft 365**. Your Microsoft 365 admin approves Softbooq once |
| **Custom** | Your mail server's **SMTP Host**, **Port**, **Username** and **Password**, and whether it uses SSL/TLS |

Send yourself a test with **Test Connection**, then choose **Confirm Configuration**.

The change takes effect at once: every email from then on goes through the new provider. If a provider was already saved, Softbooq asks you to confirm before replacing it.

### When the free emails run out

With **Default**, the screen shows how many of this month's free emails you have used. Once they are used up, further emails are **Held** rather than lost. Connect a provider, then send them again from **Email Activity**.

## See what was sent

**Email Activity** lists the latest 500 emails from your workspace: when, to whom, the subject, what kind of email it was, and its status (**Sent**, **Pending**, **Failed** or **Held**). Search by recipient or subject.

**Sent** means your provider accepted the email. With Brevo, you also see whether it arrived: **Delivered**, **Bounced**, **Address invalid**, **Refused**, **Marked as spam** or **Delayed**.

Admins can **Send again** a failed or held email, or send all held emails at once. They go out within 5 minutes.
