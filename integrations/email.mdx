Softbooq emails invoices, quotes, payment reminders and notifications on your behalf. Out of the box it sends them for you, up to a monthly limit. To send from your own address or send more, connect your own mail server.

Everything is in **Settings → Messaging & Channels → Email Service**.

## Who the email comes from

Under **Notification Email**, set the **Display Name** your customers see, and the **From Address** for providers that send from an address you choose. Left empty, the display name is your workspace's name. With **Your own domain**, the **From name** and **From address** are on that provider's own panel, beside the domain, and Softbooq offers `billing@` on your domain. **Reply-to address** is where your customers' replies go; left empty, they go to your workspace's email.

Sign-in codes, invitations and notifications to your team always come from Softbooq's own address, whichever provider you choose. Your provider sends what goes to your customers and suppliers: invoices, quotes, reminders, statements and receipts.

## Choose a provider

Under **Choose Email Provider**:

| Provider | What you enter |
|----------|----------------|
| **Default** | Nothing. Softbooq sends for you, up to a number of free emails each month |
| **Your own domain** | Your domain, like `yourcompany.com`. Softbooq shows the DNS records to add at your domain provider; once **Check records** finds them, choose a **From address** on that domain, like `billing@yourcompany.com`. Softbooq sends for you, from your address |
| **Brevo** | An **API Key** from your own Brevo account (Brevo has a free plan), and a **From address** that is a verified sender there. When Brevo asks how long the key should last, choose **No expiration**, or note the date: emails stop when it expires |
| **Custom SMTP (advanced)** | Your mail server's **SMTP Host**, **Port** (465), **Username** and **Password**. Ports 25 and 587 cannot be used from Softbooq's servers |

Send yourself a test with **Test Connection**: it uses what is on screen, including an API key or password you have typed but not saved yet, so a wrong one shows before you save. Then choose **Confirm Configuration** to save.

A saved API key or password is kept where only Softbooq's servers can read it. The field shows that one is stored, never the value, and nobody in the workspace can read it back. To change it, type a new one and save.

The change takes effect at once: every email from then on goes through the new provider. If a provider was already saved, Softbooq asks you to confirm before replacing it.

### The language of an email

An email to a customer or supplier is written in their language when Softbooq knows it, and in your workspace's language otherwise. The frame around it, down to the line at the bottom, follows the same language.

### When the free emails run out

With **Default**, the screen shows how many of this month's free emails you have used. Once they are used up, further emails are **Held** rather than lost. Connect a provider, then send them again from **Email Activity**.

## See what was sent

**Email Activity** lists the latest 500 emails from your workspace: when, to whom, the subject, what kind of email it was, and its status (**Sent**, **Pending**, **Failed** or **Held**). Search by recipient or subject.

**Sent** means your provider accepted the email. With Brevo, you also see whether it arrived: **Delivered**, **Bounced**, **Address invalid**, **Refused**, **Marked as spam** or **Delayed**.

Admins can **Send again** a failed or held email, or send all held emails at once. They go out within 5 minutes.
