If your policies and how-to pages live in Confluence, Softbooq can bring them in. Pages from the spaces you link become policies in Compliance, and they are listed in the IT knowledge base, each with a link back to Confluence. Softbooq only reads Confluence: it never changes anything there.

Confluence is in **Settings → Integrations**, under **Project Management**. Compliance and IT & Helpdesk are part of Softbooq Pro.

## Connect and link spaces

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Connect Confluence">
    Sign in to Atlassian and allow Softbooq to read your Confluence site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Manage spaces">
    **Link Confluence Spaces** lists your spaces. For each one you want, pick the policy category its pages should go to (**HR**, **Finance**, **Data protection**, **Security**, **Code of conduct**, **General** or **Other**), tick **IT Helpdesk KB** if it is a helpdesk space, and choose **Link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Linking a space brings its pages in straight away.

## What you get

- **In Compliance**: each page becomes a policy in the category you chose, with a Confluence badge. A current page is **Active**, a draft is **Draft**, and an archived page is **Retired**. Filter the **Policies** tab by source to see only Confluence pages.
- **In IT & Helpdesk**: the **Knowledge Base** tab lists the pages under **Confluence Pages**, each opening in Confluence.
- **For Jacinta**: the pages can be used when she answers questions about your policies.

## Staying in step

Pages are brought in again every night, so changes in Confluence reach Softbooq by the next morning. **Sync now** brings them in at once.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops the sync. Your space links are kept, paused, and policies already brought in stay in Softbooq. You can reconnect later.
