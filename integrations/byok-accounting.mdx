If your accountant works in lexoffice, Pennylane, sevdesk, MoneyBird, Zoho Books or DATEV, Softbooq can send your invoices there. With lexoffice, Pennylane and Zoho Books it also brings back customers and payments, so an invoice paid in that tool is marked paid in Softbooq. For Xero and QuickBooks Online, see [Xero and QuickBooks Online](/integrations/xero).

They are all in **Settings → Integrations**, under **Accounting**. You need to be an Admin to connect one.

## What each one does

| Tool | How you connect | Sends invoices | Brings back customers | Brings back payments |
|------|-----------------|:--------------:|:---------------------:|:--------------------:|
| lexoffice | Paste an **API Key** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Pennylane | Paste a **Personal Access Token** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Zoho Books | Sign in to Zoho | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| sevdesk | Paste an **API Token** | Yes | No | No |
| MoneyBird | Paste a **Personal Access Token** | Yes | No | No |
| DATEV | Request access, then sign in to DATEV | Yes | No | No |

Customers coming back are matched to your existing customers by email; anyone new becomes a customer in Softbooq. A payment coming back marks the Softbooq invoice **Paid** and posts it to your books.

## Connect a tool with a key

lexoffice, Pennylane, sevdesk and MoneyBird connect with a key you create in that tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the key in the accounting tool">
    - **lexoffice**: Einstellungen → Public API
    - **Pennylane**: Settings → API → Personal access tokens
    - **sevdesk**: Settings → User → API token (32 characters)
    - **MoneyBird**: Settings → API tokens
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its tile in Settings → Integrations">
    Choose **Connect lexoffice** (or Pennylane, sevdesk, MoneyBird), paste the key and confirm. The key is stored encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Test connection">
    It confirms the key works. To change a key later, choose **Disconnect** and connect again with the new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Zoho Books

Choose **Connect Zoho Books** and sign in to Zoho. Softbooq finds your Zoho region by itself. The panel then shows the **Organization** you connected.

## Connect DATEV

DATEV is a paid add-on. Click the DATEV tile, tell us in **Reason (optional)** why you need it, and **Submit request**. We review requests within one business day and email you when it is approved. You can then choose **Connect DATEV** and sign in. DATEV receives invoices only once they have left draft.

## When invoices are sent

Nothing moves straight after connecting. Invoices go across:

- every night, or
- when you choose **Push invoices** in the tool's panel

The first time, Softbooq sends invoices dated in the last 90 days. After that it sends new ones. Each invoice is sent once, and each run sends up to 100, oldest first. Credit notes are not sent.

With lexoffice, Pennylane and Zoho Books, the nightly run also brings back payments and customers. You can do that yourself at any time with **Pull payments** and **Sync contacts**.

## Tax codes and cost centres

Under each panel, **Integration Mappings** has **Tax Codes** and **Cost Centers** tabs: choose which code in the accounting tool each Softbooq tax rate and cost centre becomes, then **Save mappings**.

## When something does not go across

- The panel shows the last error in red under **Invoice push**, and the tile shows **Error**.
- Your Dashboard lists each record that could not be sent and why, with buttons to **Open integration**, **Open mappings** or **Reconnect**. Correct the record and send it again.

If **Test connection** says **Saved key is no longer valid - reconnect.**, the key was revoked or expired in the accounting tool: disconnect and connect again with a new key.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops sending invoices. Invoices already in the accounting tool stay there, and you can reconnect later.
