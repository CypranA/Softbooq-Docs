Connect HubSpot and your contacts and deals stay in step with Softbooq. Customers sync both ways, quotes and deals follow each other through the stages, and a deal marked won in HubSpot becomes an invoice in Softbooq. Your sales team keeps working in HubSpot while the money side stays in Softbooq.

HubSpot is in **Settings → Integrations**, under **CRM**. CRM is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## Connect

Choose **Connect HubSpot** and sign in to HubSpot. The first sync starts straight away, and after that everything syncs every night. You can sync at any time with **Sync Contacts**, **Sync Deals** and **Push Activity**.

## Choose how contacts map

**Sync mode** decides how HubSpot's records become Softbooq customers:

- **Contact per record**: each HubSpot contact becomes a customer
- **Account + contacts**: each HubSpot company becomes an account, with its contacts under it

## What syncs

| What | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Contacts | Both ways. HubSpot contacts are matched to your customers, first by their HubSpot link and then by email; customers not yet in HubSpot are added there |
| Quotes and deals | Both ways, stage by stage (see below). A stage changed in HubSpot reaches Softbooq straight away |
| Won deals | A deal marked won in HubSpot creates a draft invoice in Softbooq |
| Activity | Sent and paid invoices and accepted quotes are added as notes on the contact's timeline in HubSpot |

Contacts in HubSpot also receive your Softbooq revenue figures for them, so your sales team can see what each customer has spent.

### How quotes and deal stages match

| Quote in Softbooq | Deal stage in HubSpot |
|-------------------|-----------------------|
| Draft | Presentation scheduled |
| Sent | Contract sent |
| Accepted | Closed won |
| Declined or Expired | Closed lost |

## When a contact asks to be deleted

If a contact is deleted in HubSpot for privacy reasons, Softbooq anonymises the matching customer: the name becomes "Deleted Contact" and the link to HubSpot is removed. Their invoices stay in your books.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops the sync. Contacts already in both systems are not deleted.

## Other CRMs

Salesforce, Pipedrive and Zoho CRM connect from the same **CRM** group:

- **Salesforce**: contacts, opportunities and tasks, every night. You can create an invoice automatically when an opportunity is won, and settle differences in **Sync Conflicts** with **Use ERP** or **Use Salesforce**.
- **Pipedrive**: contacts, deals, activities, leads, products and projects, every hour, with contacts, deals and activities updated as they change.
- **Zoho CRM**: contacts, deals, activities, leads and products, every hour, with contacts and deals updated as they change.
