If your team plans its work in Asana or Trello, link those projects to Softbooq projects. Tasks come into Softbooq, where they sit next to the project's budget, time and billing, and your changes go back to the tool.

<Note>
  **Coming soon.** Microsoft Planner, Jira, Linear, Zoho Projects and Confluence show **Coming soon** in Settings and cannot be connected yet. What this page says about them describes how they will work.
</Note>


The tools are in **Settings → Integrations**, under **Project Management**. Projects is part of Softbooq Plus and Pro.

## Connect a tool

| Tool | How you connect |
|------|-----------------|
| **Microsoft Planner** | **Connect Microsoft Planner**. Each person connects their own Microsoft account, and sees their own plans |
| **Asana** | **Connect with Asana (OAuth)**, or paste a **Personal Access Token** and **Connect Asana** |
| **Trello** | **Connect Trello**, then **Allow** in the Trello window. If the window is blocked, paste a token instead |
| **Jira** | **Connect with Jira (OAuth)**, for Jira Cloud |
| **Linear** | **Connect with Linear** |
| **Zoho Projects** | **Connect Zoho Projects** |

## Link projects

Open a Softbooq project. Above its tabs is a tile for each connected tool: click it, pick the project, board, plan or team, and choose **Link**.

To link many at once, use the tool's panel in Settings: **Bulk-link projects** (Planner, Asana, Jira), **Manage Boards** (Trello) or **Bulk-link teams** (Linear). You can pair existing Softbooq projects, or create Softbooq projects from the tool's own.

A linked project can be set to sync both ways, **Pull only** or **Push only**. Zoho Projects is always pull only.

## What comes in, and what goes back

| Tool | Comes into Softbooq | Goes back to the tool |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Planner** | Tasks with their status, due date, assignee, priority, description and checklist; comments and attachments | New tasks, changes, comments and attachments |
| **Asana** | Tasks with notes, due date, status, assignee and followers; comments, attachments and dependencies | New tasks, changes and comments |
| **Trello** | Cards with description, due date, members, labels, checklists and comments | Changes to cards already linked |
| **Jira** | Issues, comments and worklogs | Changes to issues already linked |
| **Linear** | Issues, comments and sub-issues | Changes to issues already linked |
| **Zoho Projects** | Tasks with their title, status and due date | Nothing |

People are matched by email. Someone in the tool who is not in Softbooq is left unassigned. Planner lists the assignees it could not match, and Asana tells you how many comments it skipped because their author is not in Softbooq.

### Asana time, time off and teams

With Asana you can also bring in **time entries** (on Asana's Advanced tier and up), **time off**, which becomes approved leave, and **teams**. They come in with each sync, or at once with **Sync time entries**, **Sync time off** and **Sync teams**.

### Jira worklogs and IT tickets

Jira worklogs become time entries in Softbooq, waiting for approval, for people whose email matches an employee. Choose whether imported hours are billable and which cost centre they go to when you link.

A Jira project can feed **IT Helpdesk** instead of a project: choose it under **Sync issues to**, and its issues arrive as helpdesk tickets. IT Helpdesk is part of Softbooq Pro.

### Zoho Projects

Every sync brings in all your Zoho projects, up to 200, creating a Softbooq project for each one that is not linked yet.

## How often

Changes come in every 15 minutes (Zoho Projects every hour), and your changes go back within a couple of minutes. **Sync now** on a linked project syncs it straight away.

Asana, Trello and Jira can also send changes the moment they happen: on the linked project, choose **Enable real-time** (Asana), **Webhook** (Trello) or **Refresh webhook** (Jira). Linear does this by itself.

## Unlink or disconnect

**Unlink** on a project stops its sync. **Disconnect** in Settings stops the tool's sync. Tasks already in Softbooq stay in both cases.
